## Delivered scope

Base-only X3: a closed portfolio schema, repository-side semantic validator,
synthetic two-application fixture, concrete chat demonstration, and private
handoff templates. No new skill, plugin, MCP, cron, bootstrap, executable
workspace resource, or external action capability is declared.

The example contains 12 requirements, applicant essay revision history,
independent transcript/test/recommendation evidence, an institution fee waiver,
one receipted submission and official admission decision, and an unreceipted
submission with four owned gaps. The portfolio remains blocked. The example
and screenshot consistently describe two applications, not six.

Validation checks internal evidence consistency, reference coverage, revision
lineage, independent issuers, exact receipt subjects, current decision evidence,
and blocker indexes. It does not authenticate supplied documents, verify consent,
guarantee redaction, establish eligibility, evaluate essays, predict admission or
aid, or authorize action. Conflicting intake remains a human review task; do not
discard evidence to make the structured artifact pass. The synthetic deadline
test checks fixture wall times across the Los Angeles daylight-saving change;
it is not an independent verification of institutional deadlines.

## Review and release boundary

Independent in-session manual review found two actionable gaps: requirements
could borrow another subject's receipt, and contradictory admission decisions
could remain unflagged. Both were fixed with schema-valid rejection tests and
a valid owned-conflict control. The scoped re-review reported no actionable
findings. A subsequent fixture-only correction aligned the November deadline's
offset with America/Los_Angeles and added the twentieth focused test.

Standalone Codex review remains unavailable: the native CLI is absent and the
previous WSL attempt failed expired/reused-token authentication. No login,
provider, or proxy configuration was changed. The review above is manual, not a
successful standalone CLI review.

This proof does not publish to ClawHub, authorize merge, enable Claws by default,
or establish live model/provider quality. The draft still requires exact-head
CI and maintainer review.
