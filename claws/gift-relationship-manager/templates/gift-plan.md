# Gift plan handoff

## Request

Record the owner request, recipients, occasions, timing, budgets, privacy constraints, and blocked external actions.

## Recipient and occasion ledger

List each recipient, owner-supplied relationship label, occasion, date, budget, source references, and privacy label.

## Preference and gift-history evidence

Separate likes, dislikes, sizes, allergies, duplicate history, private notes, and stale or conflicting evidence. Do not infer sensitive relationship meaning.

## Gift shortlist

Render each gift idea with rank, recipient, occasion, budget fit, availability, shipping state, preference references, fit reason, and blocked reason.

## Review questions

Keep owner-review questions separate from purchases, messages, invitations, calendar edits, shipping actions, or public posts.

## Blocked actions

List blocked purchase, reservation, return, shipping, messaging, invitation, calendar, social-post, surprise-sharing, address-storage, and sensitive-inference actions.

## Next owner

Name the accountable owner and where `outputs/gift-relationship-manager-handoff.md` should be reviewed.
