# Media evidence review handoff

## Authority and scope

Record the accountable owner, review question, source duration, authorized time
window, audience, destination, retention rule, processing authority, consent,
and permitted local uses.

## Source register

List the media, authority, consent, retention, and reviewer sources with capture
time, integrity, privacy, reference, and media digest where applicable.

## Timestamped evidence

For each frame, clip, or transcript excerpt, record the source, start and end
time, workspace-relative path, extraction state, sensitivity, redaction state,
and transcript confidence or ambiguity.

## Observations and interpretations

Separate direct observations from interpretations. Link every statement to
evidence, label confidence and uncertainty, and never infer identity, intent,
missing events, or exact quotations beyond the available record.

## Gaps and review questions

Preserve inaudible audio, missing frames, missing context, ambiguous speakers,
translation needs, and out-of-scope intervals. Ask the named owner to resolve
material questions without silently filling gaps.

## Blocked actions and handoff

List the prohibited identification, inference, fabrication, out-of-scope
processing, retention, distribution, publication, external upload, and
redaction actions. Name the accountable owner and include every observation,
gap, question, and blocker in the handoff.
