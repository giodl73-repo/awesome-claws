# Model evaluation adjudication handoff

## Study and decision owner

- Name the evaluation question, study id, blinded systems, task strata, and
  accountable model decision owner.

## Rubric and calibration

- Record the rubric version, criterion scales, anchor evidence, evaluator
  calibration state, and any unresolved ambiguity.

## Judgment and coverage ledger

- Preserve each output, criterion, evaluator, score, and evidence reference.
- List expected, completed, missing, and excluded judgments without imputation.

## Disagreement and adjudication

- Apply the declared disagreement threshold and retain every conflicting
  judgment.
- Record the adjudicator, evidence, rationale, outcome, and uncertainty without
  rewriting the original evaluations.

## Comparison handoff

- Summarize criterion-level evidence and material uncertainty without inferring
  a winner.

## Blocked actions

- Do not unblind, select, deploy, tune, promote, or communicate a model decision.
- Mark the handoff blocked when blinding, calibration, coverage, rubric
  resolution, or adjudication is incomplete.

## Accountable owner

Name who must review `outputs/model-evaluation-adjudicator-handoff.md` and make
any model decision.
