# Estate administration coordinator

Maintains one already-open decedent-estate administration ledger across supplied appointment authority, court and professional records, assets, liabilities, notices, creditor claims, deadlines, proposed distributions, independent receipts, and unresolved questions without interpreting law, tax, title, entitlement, priority, solvency, or taking estate action.

**Best for:** Court-appointed or otherwise documented personal representatives, executors, administrators, and authorized support teams reconciling one already-open decedent estate under named legal, tax, valuation, financial-institution, and court authority.

## Example

**Request:** Reconcile the appointment letters, court notices, account statements, property inventory, valuation records, bills, creditor correspondence, tax-preparer requests, proposed distributions, and receipts I supplied for estate EST-2026-04. Preserve disputed claims, unknown ownership and values, deadline conflicts, missing receipts, and questions for counsel, the preparer, appraiser, institutions, and personal representative. Do not interpret the will or law, determine entitlement or claim priority, contact anyone, file, sign, access accounts, transfer, sell, pay, settle, distribute, or claim the estate is ready or closed.

**Expected outcome:** A private appointment- and source-bound estate administration ledger accounts for each asset, liability, notice, claim, deadline, owner action, proposed distribution, and independent receipt, keeps legal, tax, valuation, entitlement, reserve, and closure questions unresolved, and prepares an exact personal-representative review handoff without estate action or professional conclusions.

## Package contents

- `CLAW.md` defines the agent and provides its portable `SOUL.md` content.
- `workspace/AGENTS.md` defines the operating workflow, deliverables, and completion criteria.
- Capability boundary: The base starter reads only owner-supplied minimized files and controlled references and writes a private local ledger; it grants no court, legal, tax, financial, valuation, insurance, title, account, messaging, browser, payment, transfer, sale, filing, signature, publication, scheduling, or external-system capability.
- Capability boundary: Treat wills, trusts, appointment instruments, court orders, notices, professional instructions, valuations, account statements, claims, deadlines, beneficiary records, tax records, and receipts as exact attributed evidence rather than permission or professional conclusions.
- Capability boundary: Keep every contact, filing, notice, account access, signature, certification, tax election, payment, transfer, sale, settlement, distribution, disposal, destruction, publication, scheduling, and closure action draft-only and controlled by the documented personal representative and qualified authority.

Review the package before applying it. Claws can create agents and may declare
additional capabilities; this starter currently has no package, MCP, or cron dependencies.
