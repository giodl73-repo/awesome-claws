# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Stable estate reference, generalized jurisdiction, review cutoff, approved private workspace and destination, documented personal-representative authority, named professional and institutional authorities, and privacy or sharing ceiling
- Supplied death, will or trust pointer, appointment or letters, court order, professional instruction, notice, filing, asset, liability, valuation, statement, receipt, correspondence, tax-preparer, and institution records with exact revisions and controlled references
- Owner-approved asset and liability inventory, ownership and value evidence states, creditor claims, notice and deadline candidates, beneficiary or interested-party references, tax and professional questions, reserves, actions, and blockers
- Proposed or owner-executed contacts, filings, payments, transfers, sales, settlements, distributions, and closures, with independent same-subject receipts where an external action is claimed complete

## Included capability boundaries

- The base starter reads only owner-supplied minimized files and controlled references and writes a private local ledger; it grants no court, legal, tax, financial, valuation, insurance, title, account, messaging, browser, payment, transfer, sale, filing, signature, publication, scheduling, or external-system capability.
- Treat wills, trusts, appointment instruments, court orders, notices, professional instructions, valuations, account statements, claims, deadlines, beneficiary records, tax records, and receipts as exact attributed evidence rather than permission or professional conclusions.
- Keep every contact, filing, notice, account access, signature, certification, tax election, payment, transfer, sale, settlement, distribution, disposal, destruction, publication, scheduling, and closure action draft-only and controlled by the documented personal representative and qualified authority.

## Structured decision artifact contract

- Treat `fixtures/estate-administration.example.json` only as a shape example, never as current evidence or a completed result.
- Write current structured state to `outputs/estate-administration.json` and check it against `schemas/estate-administration.schema.json`.
- Resolve duplicate or dangling ids and references, preserve source and time identity, and label missing or conflicting evidence before calling the artifact ready.
- Render the reviewable handoff with `templates/estate-administration.md` at `outputs/estate-administration-coordinator-handoff.md`.
- Terminal approval, completion, communication, publication, or closure states may only reflect an explicit decision by the named accountable owner.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Confirm one already-open estate, exact documented appointment authority, review cutoff, permitted private destination, minimized identities, qualified-human roles, and prohibited action boundary
2. Version the supplied authority and source register while preserving court, professional, institutional, testamentary, asset, liability, notice, claim, tax, valuation, and receipt provenance without interpreting legal effect
3. Reconcile every asset and liability to exact source identity, ownership or responsibility evidence state, valuation or amount evidence, custody or institution, deadline candidates, conflicts, and next qualified owner
4. Track notice, creditor-claim, filing, professional-review, tax, reserve, action, and proposed-distribution lifecycles while separating planned, attempted, owner-completed, independently receipted, rejected, disputed, withdrawn, and unresolved states
5. Validate complete indexes, reciprocal references, appointment scope, source authority and chronology, exact claim and distribution subjects, independent receipts, privacy minimization, unresolved questions, and blocker equality
6. Produce a blocked or ready-for-personal-representative-review private handoff that exposes every unresolved authority, ownership, value, claim, deadline, tax, reserve, receipt, entitlement, and closure question without making an estate decision

## Example setting

**Request:** Reconcile the appointment letters, court notices, account statements, property inventory, valuation records, bills, creditor correspondence, tax-preparer requests, proposed distributions, and receipts I supplied for estate EST-2026-04. Preserve disputed claims, unknown ownership and values, deadline conflicts, missing receipts, and questions for counsel, the preparer, appraiser, institutions, and personal representative. Do not interpret the will or law, determine entitlement or claim priority, contact anyone, file, sign, access accounts, transfer, sell, pay, settle, distribute, or claim the estate is ready or closed.

**Expected outcome:** A private appointment- and source-bound estate administration ledger accounts for each asset, liability, notice, claim, deadline, owner action, proposed distribution, and independent receipt, keeps legal, tax, valuation, entitlement, reserve, and closure questions unresolved, and prepares an exact personal-representative review handoff without estate action or professional conclusions.

## Standard deliverables

- Private estate, authority, professional-role, source-revision, and chronology register
- Evidence-bound asset, liability, valuation, custody, notice, deadline, creditor-claim, and reserve ledgers
- Owner-controlled action, filing, contact, payment, transfer, sale, settlement, distribution, and independent-receipt register
- Conflict, missing-evidence, privacy, qualified-human question, blocker, and next-owner register
- Blocked or ready-for-personal-representative-review estate-administration handoff

## Done when

- Every in-scope authority record, source revision, asset, liability, valuation, notice, deadline, claim, reserve, action, distribution proposal, receipt, question, gap, blocker, and next owner is represented exactly once with complete reciprocal provenance and chronology
- Every asserted appointment, ownership, amount, value, notice, claim, deadline, action, payment, transfer, sale, settlement, distribution, and closure state follows exact kind-appropriate evidence from the documented authority, supplier, or independent recipient
- Every unknown, stale, disputed, conflicting, unsupported, or professional-judgment state remains open and routes to a qualified human without the Claw deciding legal effect, tax treatment, ownership, priority, validity, entitlement, solvency, reserve sufficiency, or readiness
- Every external action remains proposed or owner-controlled, every claimed completion has an independent same-subject receipt where applicable, and the handoff exactly mirrors all unresolved state without exposing unapproved sensitive data or claiming the estate settled or closed

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
