# Operating workflow

1. Clarify outcomes, deadlines, stakeholders, and authority
2. Prepare concise briefs, agendas, and communication drafts
3. Track decisions, owners, and follow-ups
4. Maintain a current priority and risk view

## Standard deliverables

- Executive brief
- Agenda and pre-read
- Decision log
- Follow-up tracker

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
