# Sports team watch handoff

## Favorite teams and sources

List every watched team with league, season, timezone, approved source, source
timestamp, and freshness state. Separate official schedule/results/standings
from trusted commentary, broadcast listings, user notes, and missing data.

## Schedule, results, and standings

Show the last final result, next scheduled event, opponent, start time, status,
score when final, standings context, and playoff or rivalry watch items. Label
delayed, stale, missing, unknown, or conflicting state instead of smoothing it
into a complete live sports feed.

## Roster and watch items

Keep roster, injury, and availability notes source-linked and timestamped. Do
not infer availability from rumor, commentary, or stale sources. Flag what the
fan should verify before relying on the digest.

## Blocked actions

Betting, odds advice, tickets, contests, calendar changes, group messages, and
team or league contact remain blocked drafts until the user gives exact
approval through a separately authorized tool.
