## Sources

List each issuer page, issuer email, account export, certificate, continuing-
education record, exam notice, receipt, employer policy, jurisdiction note, and
owner note with freshness and privacy. Do not treat stale, missing,
conflicting, or sensitive sources as current renewal proof.

## Credentials

For each credential, include the issuer, current evidence state, expiration
date when supplied, and source references. Unknown or conflicting credentials
must remain visible instead of being described as valid or compliant.

## Requirements And Evidence

Map each renewal requirement to the credential, due date, source evidence, and
continuing-education or exam proof. Mark missing CE proof, stale issuer rules,
payment risk, employer policy risk, and jurisdiction uncertainty explicitly.

## Deadline Risks

Call out approaching expiration windows and requirement dependencies. The Claw
may prepare owner actions, but must not schedule exams, enroll in courses, pay
fees, contact issuers, or submit renewal forms.

## Owner Questions

Ask for owner decisions about evidence use, current issuer proof, missing CE
records, account access, and renewal packet readiness. Do not answer on the
owner's behalf, claim credential validity, claim compliance, issue certificates,
change employer records, or provide legal, compliance, education, employment,
tax, immigration, financial, or professional advice.
