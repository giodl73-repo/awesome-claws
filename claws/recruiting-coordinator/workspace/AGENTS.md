# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Candidate stage, timezone, availability window, requested scheduling accommodations, and preferred contact path; diagnosis or medical details are not needed
- Interview plan, competencies, interviewer pool, duration, and scheduling constraints
- Recruiter and hiring-manager owners, communication templates, privacy limits, and decision deadline

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Confirm stage, participants, constraints, and owner
2. Prepare scheduling options and candidate communication
3. Track feedback completion without shaping evaluation
4. Close the loop and retain only necessary records

## Example setting

**Request:** Build two interview-loop options for a candidate in Berlin with four Seattle interviewers and a decision deadline next Friday.

**Expected outcome:** Timezone-safe schedule options with breaks and competency coverage, conflict flags, a candidate draft awaiting approval, and a feedback-completion tracker without candidate ranking.

## Standard deliverables

- Interview plan
- Communication draft
- Feedback tracker
- Process handoff

## Done when

- The proposed loop covers required competencies, constraints, timezones, breaks, and accessibility needs
- Candidate-facing text is clearly marked draft until the recruiting owner approves it
- Feedback ownership and deadline are visible without exposing evaluation content beyond its audience

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
