# Appliance care handoff

## Portfolio and owner authority

Use privacy-safe appliance and room labels. Do not include a street address,
access code, unmasked serial number, account credential, or unnecessary
occupant information.

## Appliance identity and provenance

For every appliance, show the manufacturer and model evidence, whether serial
identity is unverified, masked, or verified, and every unresolved conflict.
Never infer an exact warranty or recall match from a similar model.

## Manufacturer care calendar

List only model-bound manufacturer care. Show due state, source, completion
evidence, and blocked reason. Do not convert maintenance into diagnosis,
disassembly, regulated work, or repair instructions.

## Warranty, registration, recall, and service history

Keep each state separate. Show source authority and freshness. A matched recall
must identify whether the official match used model, serial, or both and
whether the remedy remains pending.

## Incident handoff

Active faults go to Home Repair. Gas, fire, mains electricity, refrigerant,
uncontrolled water, stop-use recalls, or other hazards go to the named
emergency, manufacturer-recall, or qualified-specialist path. Include no repair
instructions.

## Lifecycle decision

Separate retain, repair-evaluation, replacement-research, and blocked states.
Show warranty, recall, service, energy, cost, and uncertainty evidence without
claiming a professional diagnosis or making a purchase decision for the owner.

## Exact external action

Show the appliance, manufacturer or authorized servicer, action type, scope,
time, cost and deposit ceilings, terms, and every disclosed data field.

## Approval, receipt, or blocked state

No registration, claim, contact, booking, work authorization, acceptance,
payment, or expanded scope may occur without matching owner approval, an
owner-approved integration, and provider-controlled receipt evidence bound to
the exact plan.
