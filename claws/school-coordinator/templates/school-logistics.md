# School Logistics Ledger

Summarize the guardian, school window, privacy mode, timezone, and as-of timestamp.

## Items

List each assignment, form, event, supply item, teacher note, and deadline with student-safe label, course label, due date, state, guardian action, privacy label, source references, and blocked reason.

## Evidence

Show each source with kind, authority, captured time, freshness, privacy label, and reference.

## Privacy

Show which student, accommodation, school, class, location, and guardian-only details are minimized, redacted, or reserved for guardian review.

## Accommodation And Conflicts

Show accommodation evidence, possible conflicts, unclear due dates, stale or missing portal evidence, and unsupported claims without submitting forms, messaging teachers, contacting schools, paying fees, changing enrollment, changing attendance, or editing calendars.

## Guardian Review

Keep guardian-review questions separate from form submission, teacher or school contact, payments, calendar edits, enrollment changes, attendance changes, student-detail disclosure, or education, medical, legal, and discipline decisions.

## Blocked Actions

List blocked form, teacher-message, school-contact, payment, calendar, enrollment, attendance, disclosure, education-decision, medical-decision, legal-decision, and discipline-decision actions.
