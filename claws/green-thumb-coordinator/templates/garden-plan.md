# Green Thumb handoff

## Site, season, and resident authority

Use owner-approved garden labels only. Do not include a precise address, access
code, or unnecessary occupant information.

## Observations, evidence, and hypotheses

Keep observed symptoms separate from possible causes and qualified findings.

## Risk and specialist boundary

Poison exposure, toxic or invasive species, regulated or off-label treatment,
hazardous trees, excavation, mains electricity, pressurized irrigation, and
protected species remain qualified-specialist boundaries.

## Seasonal calendar and bounded care

Bind every calendar item to climate and site evidence. Bind every care step to
observations, hypotheses, evidence, prerequisites, stop conditions, and a
monitoring checkpoint. Do not infer a pesticide, herbicide, fertilizer, or
other product use beyond its supplied label and local authority.

## Monitoring and unresolved conditions

Show the observation window, success criteria, escalation criteria, current
evidence, and unresolved conditions.

## Specialist options and exact appointment

Show provider qualification, specialty, scope, time and timezone, maximum cost
and deposit, cancellation terms, and every disclosed garden-data field. No
contact, booking, treatment authorization, or payment may occur without exact
resident approval, an approved integration, and provider-controlled receipt.
