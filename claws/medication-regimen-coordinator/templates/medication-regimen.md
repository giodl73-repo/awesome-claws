# Medication regimen coordinator handoff

## Review boundary

Name the patient or guardian authority, explicitly authorized caregivers,
review period and as-of time, privacy and sharing ceiling, approved source
classes, and the clinician, pharmacist, poison-control, or emergency route.
This artifact is private coordination evidence, not a prescription, medication
administration record from a clinical system, or a clinical decision.

## Medication and order revision register

For every medication, show only a privacy-minimized label, exact attributed
order revision, predecessor and successor lineage, current, held,
discontinued, superseded, or conflicted state, supplied directions, source,
issuer, and chronology. Preserve conflicting or stale instructions without
selecting one. Never calculate a dose or infer clinical intent.

## Planned occurrences and observations

List each owner-approved planned occurrence in the review period and keep
scheduled, observed, unknown, and not-applicable state separate. An observed
taken, not-taken, held, refused, missed, or unknown state must identify the
patient or authorized caregiver, exact order revision, observation time, and
controlled source. A blank checklist or absent note remains unknown and is not
an adherence conclusion.

## Supply, expiry, and external-action evidence

Show owner-supplied counts, units, count times, package labels, expiry evidence,
and stated supply condition without estimating clinical continuity or deciding
availability. Separate planned, attempted, failed, blocked, partial, rejected,
withdrawn, and owner-completed refill, renewal, transfer, dispensing, delivery,
pickup, or disposal-review actions. Completed actions require an independent
same-subject receipt; the Claw performs none of them.

## Attributed warnings and qualified-human questions

Render clinician, pharmacist, dispenser, poison-control, or emergency material
only as exact dated attributed evidence. Route direction conflicts, identity,
missed-dose, reaction, overdose or poison, interaction, expiry or storage,
refill or supply, and other clinical questions to the named qualified human.
Do not diagnose, interpret, identify an interaction, decide urgency, or provide
instructions.

## Gaps and next owners

Index every open identity, order-revision, conflicting-direction, observation,
supply, expiry, refill-receipt, privacy-authority, urgent-human-review, and
other evidence gap. Name the next human owner and exact supporting or
resolution evidence. Keep the review blocked while any gap, unknown occurrence,
blocked action, conflict, stale source, or unanswered qualified-human question
remains.

## Prohibited claims and actions

Repeat that the artifact makes no diagnosis, triage, treatment, dosage,
interaction, urgency, safety, effectiveness, adherence, availability,
entitlement, clinical-continuity, or closure conclusion and performs no
prescribing, dispensing, administration, medication change, contact, refill,
renewal, portal or account change, PHI disclosure, scheduling, insurance,
authorization, or payment action.
