# Pond and water feature handoff

## Site, project phase, and resident authority

Use privacy-safe site and feature labels. Do not include a street address,
access code, unneeded occupant information, account credentials, or precise
protected-species locations.

## Contained-water system and evidence

Show the liner or basin, pump, filtration, skimmer, aeration, waterfall,
plumbing, overflow, autofill, lighting, and other relevant components. Keep
proposed, installed, and unknown state distinct and cite direct equipment,
plan, installation, manufacturer, or service evidence.

## Installation readiness and bid scope

Show utility-location, permit, electrical, structural, hydraulic, drainage,
access, and environmental requirements. Missing or unknown requirements block
bid-ready or complete state. The artifact may prepare a quote scope but must
not direct excavation, wiring, structural work, pressurized plumbing, or other
qualified work.

## Risk and owner-system handoffs

Route system faults and leaks to Home Repair, terrestrial plant work to Green
Thumb, fish illness or treatment to Pet Care or a veterinarian, and invasive,
protected, contamination, discharge, or public-safety concerns to the
appropriate qualified authority.

## Seasonal operations

Bind each operation to installed components and current manufacturer,
extension, regulator, laboratory, or qualified-specialist evidence. Name
conditions and stop conditions. Do not convert routine care into repair,
chemical treatment, stocking, organism release, or animal-health instructions.

## Water quality and habitat observations

Show measurement time, evidence, unit, authoritative threshold source, and
whether the result is within range, outside range, or unknown. Keep habitat
observations separate from species identity, diagnosis, treatment, and outcome.

## Specialist options and exact appointment

Show provider qualification, specialty, scope, time and timezone, maximum cost
and deposit, cancellation terms, and every disclosed site-data field.

## Approval, receipt, or blocked state

No contact, booking, work or treatment authorization, organism release,
payment, or expanded scope may occur without matching resident approval, an
approved integration, and provider-controlled receipt evidence bound to the
exact plan.
