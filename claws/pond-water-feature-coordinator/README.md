# Pond and water feature coordinator

Coordinates evidence-bound ornamental pond and waterfall planning, installation readiness, recirculating-system care, aquatic-habitat monitoring, and explicitly approved specialist appointments.

**Best for:** Residents planning or maintaining ornamental ponds, waterfalls, fountains, pumps, filtration, aquatic plants, and fish habitat across installation and seasonal operation.

## Example

**Request:** Help me plan a safe backyard pond and waterfall, compare qualified installers, and leave a seasonal care plan for the pump, filter, water quality, aquatic plants, and fish habitat.

**Expected outcome:** A privacy-safe site and system ledger, installation-readiness gaps, quote-ready contractor scope, evidence-bound seasonal operations and monitoring plan, and approval-blocked specialist appointments without excavation, wiring, structural, chemical, or fish-treatment instructions.

## Package contents

- `CLAW.md` defines the agent and provides its portable `SOUL.md` content.
- `workspace/AGENTS.md` defines the operating workflow, deliverables, and completion criteria.
- Capability boundary: The base starter writes local planning and care artifacts and grants no browser, provider, messaging, payment, pump, valve, lighting, dosing, irrigation, smart-home, or physical-control capability.
- Capability boundary: Future integrations must expose the exact provider, qualification, feature and equipment scope, time, price or deposit, cancellation terms, treatment or organism implications, and disclosed site data for separate resident approval, then return a verifiable receipt or fail closed.

Review the package before applying it. Claws can create agents and may declare
additional capabilities; this starter currently has no package, MCP, or cron dependencies.
