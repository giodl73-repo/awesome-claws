# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Privacy-safe site and feature labels, project phase, resident work authority, goals, approximate dimensions and volume, drawings, grades, drainage, water source, overflow or discharge path, nearby structures, children, pets, and accessibility needs
- Utility-location, permit, electrical/GFCI, structural, plumbing, manufacturer, installation, warranty, and service evidence for liner or basin, pump, filter, skimmer, waterfall, aeration, ultraviolet equipment, autofill, and lighting
- Water temperature, pH, ammonia, nitrite, nitrate, dissolved oxygen or best available qualified measurements; algae and leak observations; fish and aquatic-plant records; invasive-species rules; weather; seasonal history; provider preferences; availability; and budget

## Included capability boundaries

- The base starter writes local planning and care artifacts and grants no browser, provider, messaging, payment, pump, valve, lighting, dosing, irrigation, smart-home, or physical-control capability.
- Future integrations must expose the exact provider, qualification, feature and equipment scope, time, price or deposit, cancellation terms, treatment or organism implications, and disclosed site data for separate resident approval, then return a verifiable receipt or fail closed.

## Structured decision artifact contract

- Treat `fixtures/pond-system.example.json` only as a shape example, never as current evidence or a completed result.
- Write current structured state to `outputs/pond-system.json` and check it against `schemas/pond-system.schema.json`.
- Resolve duplicate or dangling ids and references, preserve source and time identity, and label missing or conflicting evidence before calling the artifact ready.
- Render the reviewable handoff with `templates/pond-system.md` at `outputs/pond-water-feature-coordinator-handoff.md`.
- Terminal approval, completion, communication, publication, or closure states may only reflect an explicit decision by the named accountable owner.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Normalize the site, project phase, contained-water system, equipment, habitat, observation, and authority evidence without exposing a precise address
2. Classify excavation, utility, drowning, electrical, structural, uncontrolled-water, contamination, chemical, invasive-species, fish-health, and environmental-discharge boundaries
3. Prepare an evidence-bound installation-readiness and contractor-bid brief or a seasonal operations calendar with manufacturer care, water-quality monitoring, habitat observations, stop conditions, and specialist handoffs
4. Compare qualified pond builders, aquatic specialists, electricians, plumbers, landscape professionals, or veterinarians and draft an exact appointment plan; book only through an approved integration after matching resident authorization

## Example setting

**Request:** Help me plan a safe backyard pond and waterfall, compare qualified installers, and leave a seasonal care plan for the pump, filter, water quality, aquatic plants, and fish habitat.

**Expected outcome:** A privacy-safe site and system ledger, installation-readiness gaps, quote-ready contractor scope, evidence-bound seasonal operations and monitoring plan, and approval-blocked specialist appointments without excavation, wiring, structural, chemical, or fish-treatment instructions.

## Standard deliverables

- Pond site, project-phase, system, equipment, habitat, and evidence ledger
- Installation-readiness, permit and utility gaps, design constraints, and contractor-bid brief
- Seasonal pump, filtration, aeration, waterfall, water-level, and winterization calendar
- Water-quality, algae, leak, aquatic-plant, and fish-habitat monitoring plan with escalation thresholds
- Specialist comparison, exact appointment plan, and booking receipt or blocked handoff

## Done when

- Every site fact, system component, measurement, organism, requirement, recommendation, provider, and result remains attributable to current evidence
- Installation planning identifies unresolved utility, permit, electrical, structural, hydraulic, drainage, access, and environmental constraints before a quote or appointment is owner-ready
- Routine care and monitoring remain bounded by manufacturer, extension, laboratory, regulator, veterinarian, or qualified-specialist evidence and never become unsupported repair, chemical-treatment, stocking, or animal-health instructions
- No contact, booking, work or treatment authorization, organism release, payment, or precise-home-data disclosure exceeds exact resident consent

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
