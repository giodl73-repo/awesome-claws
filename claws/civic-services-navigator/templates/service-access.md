# Civic service access packet

## Case boundary

- Need, as-of time, revision, resident owner, privacy ceiling, and prohibited authority

## Jurisdiction resolution

- Candidate jurisdictions, service-area claims, official locator evidence, conflicts, and resident confirmation

## Public-service route map

- Administering agency, official program identity and revision, service area, status, required-material state, timing windows, office details, fees, and source freshness

## Criterion evidence crosswalk

- Published criterion text, resident-controlled evidence state, controlled evidence reference, privacy, and agency-owned adjudication

## Channel matrix

- Online, in-person, phone, mail, interpreter, and accessibility-support routes with language and accessibility evidence

## Access-step graph

- Ordered step dependencies, source basis, resident owner, block state, external-action boundary, and exact completion evidence

## Agency-question docket

- Open question, agency owner, answer state, and authoritative answer source

## Versioned resident handoff

- Conflicting locality claims, unresolved questions, selected next route, next revision, and accountable resident owner

Applications, agency contact, case initiation, resident-material transmission, appointments, payments, accounts, professional advice, eligibility, approval, and denial remain outside this artifact.
