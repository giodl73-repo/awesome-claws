## Sources

List client notes, meeting summaries, referrals, email snippets, scope drafts,
proposal drafts, pricing notes, availability notes, portfolio evidence,
contract notes, invoice notes, and owner notes with freshness and privacy.
Preserve stale, missing, conflicting, and sensitive evidence as blockers or
owner-review items.

## Opportunities

For each client opportunity, show stage, priority, source evidence, and current
owner-visible blockers. Do not promote a lead into a commitment, acceptance, or
sent proposal unless the owner has already done that outside the Claw.

## Scope, Proposal, And Follow-Up Readiness

Map scope items, proposal sections, pricing, timelines, availability, case
studies, references, terms notes, and follow-up drafts to source evidence.
Drafts can be prepared for owner review, but the Claw must not send, submit,
publish, invoice, collect payment, sign, or accept work.

## Commitment Risks And Gaps

Call out unsupported prices, stale client context, missing scope, availability
conflicts, contract risk, payment risk, portfolio risk, sensitive details, and
other commitment risks.

## Owner Questions

Ask for owner decisions about pricing, scope, terms, availability, follow-up,
and readiness. Do not contact clients, quote binding terms, promise
availability, fabricate credentials or case studies, change accounts, or give
legal, tax, financial, accounting, employment, contracting, or professional
advice.
