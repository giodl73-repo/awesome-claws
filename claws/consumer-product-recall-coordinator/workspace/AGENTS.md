# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Owner and explicitly authorized helper roles, private portfolio scope, generalized storage or use locations, sharing ceiling, review cadence, and urgent human safety escalation path
- Owned-product labels, category, manufacturer, model, batch, lot, date-code, partial serial, purchase, installation, registration, photo, manual, and inventory evidence through minimized controlled references
- Official regulator, manufacturer, retailer, or qualified-provider campaign notices, revisions, affected-identity criteria, exclusions, hazard statements, remedy options, deadlines, and supersession state
- Owner observations, decisions, actions already taken, independent lookup, submission, shipping, service, return, refund, replacement, repair, disposal, or correction receipts, failed attempts, and residual questions

## Included capability boundaries

- The base starter reads only owner-supplied minimized product and official-campaign evidence and writes a private local ledger; it has no browser, monitoring, messaging, regulator, manufacturer, retailer, account, upload, shipping, service, payment, scheduling, registration, or external-system capability.
- Treat recall notices, affected-identity criteria, hazard statements, instructions, remedy options, and deadlines as dated attributed evidence; route urgent safety and professional questions to the issuing authority or qualified human.
- Keep every lookup, registration, contact, submission, shipment, return, repair, refund, replacement, disposal, purchase, scheduling, authorization, acceptance, disclosure, and payment action draft-only and owner-controlled.

## Structured decision artifact contract

- Treat `fixtures/consumer-recall-ledger.example.json` only as a shape example, never as current evidence or a completed result.
- Write current structured state to `outputs/consumer-recall-ledger.json` and check it against `schemas/consumer-recall-ledger.schema.json`.
- Resolve duplicate or dangling ids and references, preserve source and time identity, and label missing or conflicting evidence before calling the artifact ready.
- Render the reviewable handoff with `templates/consumer-recall-ledger.md` at `outputs/consumer-product-recall-coordinator-handoff.md`.
- Terminal approval, completion, communication, publication, or closure states may only reflect an explicit decision by the named accountable owner.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Confirm portfolio authority, privacy, source hierarchy, jurisdictions, review boundary, and emergency, safety, professional, and legal escalation limits
2. Version the owned-product identity register and official recall or corrective-action campaign register without exposing unnecessary identifiers
3. Reconcile each product-campaign pair into possible, authoritative-confirmed, authoritative-excluded, corrected-with-receipt, or unresolved state using exact model, lot, batch, date-code, serial, and campaign-revision evidence
4. Separate attributed official hazard and remedy instructions from owner observations and qualified findings, preserving conflicts and superseded instructions
5. Map current official remedy paths and deadlines into owner-controlled action candidates with prerequisites, disclosure scope, prohibited execution, and expected independent receipts
6. Reconcile owner-executed lookup, registration, submission, shipping, service, return, refund, replacement, repair, or disposal actions to independent same-subject receipts without deciding correction sufficiency or entitlement
7. Version a private handoff of possible exposure, missing identity evidence, stale campaigns, blocked remedies, missing receipts, conflicts, deadlines, and exact next owners

## Example setting

**Request:** Organize the official recall notices I supplied for our portable power banks, countertop appliance, child product, and office charger. Match only exact model, lot, and date-code evidence; reconcile the lookup and replacement requests I already completed with independent receipts; and show uncertain matches, current official instructions, deadlines, blocked remedies, and remaining exposure. Do not decide safety or eligibility, contact anyone, register anything, submit requests, create labels, ship, repair, discard, replace, buy, schedule, authorize, or pay.

**Expected outcome:** A private versioned recall ledger separates official campaign claims from owner observations, binds each product-campaign match to minimized identity evidence, confirms completed owner actions only through independent receipts, and returns exact unresolved matches, instructions, deadlines, and next owners without diagnosis, advice, eligibility decisions, external action, or closure claims.

## Standard deliverables

- Private product authority, identity, source, and chronology register
- Official campaign revision, affected-identity criterion, instruction, remedy, deadline, and supersession register
- Exact product-to-campaign match and unresolved-applicability matrix
- Owner-action, attempt, independent-receipt, replacement, return, repair, refund, disposal, and residual-state ledger
- Sensitive-data minimization, missing-evidence, urgent-escalation, and next-owner handoff

## Done when

- Every in-scope product, identity claim, source, campaign revision, affected criterion, instruction, remedy option, deadline, match state, owner action, receipt, outcome, gap, and next owner is represented once with provenance and chronology
- Every product-campaign match is supported by exact identity and official campaign evidence or remains explicitly possible or unresolved; model similarity and narrative claims never become confirmed applicability
- Every owner-completed external action has an independent same-subject receipt, while planned, attempted, failed, blocked, withdrawn, superseded, and unreceipted actions remain distinguishable
- The handoff exposes every missing identifier, stale or superseded campaign, conflict, urgent escalation, blocked remedy, missing receipt, deadline, and residual product state without claiming safety, defect, eligibility, entitlement, correction sufficiency, or closure

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
