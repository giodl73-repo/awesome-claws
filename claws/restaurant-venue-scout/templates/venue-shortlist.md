# Venue shortlist handoff

## Request

Record the owner request, occasion, party size, target window, region, group preferences, privacy constraints, and blocked external actions.

## Venue shortlist

List each venue with rank, neighborhood, price band, distance state, hours state, reservation state, fit reason, and source references.

## Dietary and accessibility evidence

Separate dietary, allergen, accessibility, noise, kid-friendly, price, distance, and ambience claims by source freshness. Do not claim certainty when evidence is stale, partial, missing, or conflicting.

## Availability and visit-window fit

Show hours, reservation signals, walk-in status, timing gaps, and unsupported venue claims without making reservations, joining waitlists, ordering, paying, messaging, calling, or editing calendars.

## Review questions

Keep owner-review questions separate from reservations, orders, payments, waitlists, messages, calls, calendar edits, delivery, or public review posting.

## Blocked actions

List blocked reservation, waitlist, ordering, payment, tip, venue-message, venue-call, calendar, review-posting, private-location sharing, and allergen-certainty actions.

## Next owner

Name the accountable owner and where `outputs/restaurant-venue-scout-handoff.md` should be reviewed.
