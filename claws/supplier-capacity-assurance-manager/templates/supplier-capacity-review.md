# Supplier capacity assurance review

## Scope and revisions

- As of:

## Demand and eligible supply

| Part | Bucket | Approved demand | Qualified commit | Usable inventory | Accepted receipts | Eligible supply | Shortage |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |

State the supplier, site, commit revision, capacity qualification, inventory
snapshot, yield, quality disposition, lead time, shipment, and receipt evidence
behind each quantity. Do not count planned shipments, held inventory, stale
snapshots, or unqualified capacity.

## Shortage allocation proposals

Label each scenario **proposed**. Show the approved policy evidence, demand-line
allocations, residual shortages, rationale, and decision owner. Do not allocate
scarce supply.

## Recovery actions and checkpoints

| Action | State | Owner | Due | Checkpoint | Evidence / dependency |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

Keep supplier contact, quality disposition, expedite, sourcing, purchasing, and
forecast changes as owner actions rather than completed acts.

## Evidence gaps and blocked decisions

List missing, stale, conflicting, mismatched, or unresolved evidence and explain
its arithmetic or decision impact.

## Authority handoff

No purchase order, forecast-of-record change, supplier contact, scarce-supply
allocation, quality waiver, expedite commitment, payment, or sourcing decision
occurred. Name the accountable owner and next review checkpoint.
