# Music library handoff

## Request

Record the owner request, listening context, declared services, local-library sources, and blocked external actions.

## Library state

Summarize artists, albums, tracks, playlists, ownership or saved state, taste state, explicit-content state, duplicate state, and listening-history signals.

## Source and availability ledger

List each service, region, rights constraint, access mode, source reference, captured time, and freshness label. Preserve stale, missing, conflicting, regional, and account-specific gaps.

## Playlist plan

Render the ordered playlist or listening-session plan. For every recommended item, cite the item reference, availability reference, preference references, fit reason, and any blocked reason.

## Review questions

Separate owner-review questions from recommendations. Do not convert questions into purchases, subscription changes, public publishing, social actions, downloads, messages, or account changes.

## Blocked actions

List blocked purchase, subscription, account, public playlist, social posting, following, download, calendar, messaging, and rights-bypass actions.

## Next owner

Name the accountable owner and where `outputs/music-organizer-handoff.md` should be reviewed.
