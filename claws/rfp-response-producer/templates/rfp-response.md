# RFP internal review

## Scope and baseline

Record opportunity, product/version, owner, private recipient, draft revision,
buyer baseline and amendments, as-of time and timezone-bearing deadline.

## Coverage and source notes

For each question and compound part, identify its supported answer or explicit
gap, cited source claims and applicable disclosure permission. Preserve original
identifiers, mandatory status, word limits and response values. Review the actual
draft text against the supplied source passages, not merely the citation IDs.

## Amendments and reviews

Show changed requirements and exactly which draft answers, summary passages,
attachments and prior reviews require reopening. Preserve review history without
implying that superseded approval remains effective.

## Attachments

List every required attachment, required and available revision, supplied bytes,
buyer permission and unresolved identity or disclosure checks. Never label
metadata-only records as attached files.

## Prioritized owner questions

Give the exact question, affected question identifiers, evidence and draft
revision to the responsible owner. Separate missing information from requests
for commercial, legal, security, delivery or bid judgment.

## Handoff

State remaining mandatory gaps, format conversion and owner decisions. Draft
completeness is not commercial viability. No submission, contractual acceptance,
approval or commitment is performed by this artifact.
