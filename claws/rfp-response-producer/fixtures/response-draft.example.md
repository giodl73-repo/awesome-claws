It does not meet the mandatory EU-only hosting requirement: application data, backups and logs are hosted in the US only. Availability and service credits remain pending review. No reference is authorized, no go-live date is committed, and both requested attachments are absent. This is a complete question-by-question working draft, not a submission-ready bid.
