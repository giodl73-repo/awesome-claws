# Fundraising campaign brief

## Objective, audience, and authority

Name the campaign owner, review team, approved program, date range, currency,
goal, and artifact state. Use aggregate audience criteria only; do not include
donor records or contact details.

## Approved impact claims

For each claim, record its type, exact wording, evidence references, reviewer,
allowed channels, restrictions, and support state. Keep matching, urgency,
financial-need, endorsement, restriction, and tax language blocked until its
specific evidence and reviewer approval are current.

## Offer and restriction terms

Separate approved terms from drafts or conflicts. Never infer matching ratios,
deadlines, tax treatment, restricted-fund terms, or beneficiary stories.

## Channels, accessibility, and stewardship

Map every draft asset to aggregate audiences and supported claims. Record
accessibility checks, consent requirements, suppression rules, stewardship
owners, and the fact that publication has not occurred.

## Measurement and approval gates

Define metrics without donor-level data. List unresolved questions, blocking
claims, the accountable human or team owner, included assets, and all actions
that remain prohibited.
