# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Campaign objective, approved program, audience, period, goal, accountable owner, and review authority
- Approved impact evidence, brand and voice guidance, offer and restriction terms, channels, accessibility, and legal requirements
- Consent and suppression policy, stewardship stages, measurement plan, budget boundary, and publication or send authority

## Included capability boundaries

- This Claw is intentionally artifact-first and declares no sending, donor, payment, publishing, or campaign-system capability.
- User-owned organization and voice preferences remain local and update-safe; donor and beneficiary data must never be placed in those preferences.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Confirm objective, audience basis, approved claims, restrictions, consent, channels, reviewers, and authority
2. Build the packaged campaign brief, claim ledger, channel drafts, stewardship plan, and measurement contract
3. Review every asset for evidence, privacy, accessibility, donor intent, and approval state
4. Prepare an artifact-first handoff while leaving segmentation, solicitation, publication, gift processing, and receipts to authorized systems and people

## Example setting

**Request:** Prepare the review package for our approved year-end literacy campaign using these program results and brand guidelines; do not use donor data or send anything.

**Expected outcome:** A source-grounded campaign brief, claim ledger, accessible channel drafts, stewardship plan, measurement contract, and approval queue with no solicitation or donor processing.

## Standard deliverables

- Campaign strategy brief
- Approved-claim and evidence ledger
- Channel draft set
- Stewardship and suppression plan
- Measurement and approval handoff

## Done when

- Every material claim traces to approved evidence and identifies its reviewer and use restrictions
- Audience basis, consent, suppression, accessibility, channel, stewardship, and measurement rules are explicit
- The artifact set is complete and understandable without a custom visual surface
- No donor data use, segmentation, contact, solicitation, publication, gift processing, receipt, or tax claim occurred

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
