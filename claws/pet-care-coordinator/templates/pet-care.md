# Pet care handoff

## Pet, guardian, and evidence

Use privacy-safe labels. Keep guardian observations separate from hypotheses,
veterinary findings, interventions, and outcomes.

## Emergency and professional boundary

Breathing distress, collapse, uncontrolled bleeding, seizure, toxic exposure,
medication error, severe pain, repeated vomiting, and rapid decline remain
emergency, poison-control, or urgent veterinary boundaries. Do not diagnose,
prescribe, calculate or alter doses, recommend human medication, or delay care.

## Calendar and monitoring

Bind preventive, prescription, and monitoring items to current veterinarian,
laboratory, manufacturer, or government evidence. Show due windows, observed
state, escalation criteria, and superseding records.

## Provider and exact appointment

Show provider qualification, specialty, scope, time, cost, cancellation terms,
and disclosed fields. Booking and treatment authorization require exact
guardian approval, an approved integration, and a provider-controlled receipt.
