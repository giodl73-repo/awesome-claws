# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Renter owner, authorized helpers, generalized premises reference, supplied lease and amendment set, jurisdictions, privacy ceiling, and escalation path
- Official or landlord notices, published procedures, obligations, deadlines, payment statements and receipts, deposit records, and access notices
- Condition evidence, maintenance episodes, renter reports, qualified findings, requests already sent, provider or landlord responses, attempts, and independent receipts
- Move-in or move-out evidence, keys and access-device state, owner decisions, disputed charges, unresolved questions, and next responsible humans

## Included capability boundaries

- The base starter reads owner-supplied minimized records and writes a private local artifact only
- Treat lease language, notices, procedures, findings, and landlord positions as dated attributed evidence rather than universal guidance
- Keep every contact, submission, payment, signature, appointment, access grant, repair authorization, and account change draft-only and owner-controlled

## Structured decision artifact contract

- Treat `fixtures/rental-housing-ledger.example.json` only as a shape example, never as current evidence or a completed result.
- Write current structured state to `outputs/rental-housing-ledger.json` and check it against `schemas/rental-housing-ledger.schema.json`.
- Resolve duplicate or dangling ids and references, preserve source and time identity, and label missing or conflicting evidence before calling the artifact ready.
- Render the reviewable handoff with `templates/rental-housing-ledger.md` at `outputs/rental-housing-coordinator-handoff.md`.
- Terminal approval, completion, communication, publication, or closure states may only reflect an explicit decision by the named accountable owner.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Confirm tenancy scope, supplied authority, privacy, jurisdictions, source hierarchy, and urgent safety or professional escalation boundaries
2. Version the lease, notice, payment, condition, maintenance, access, and move-state source register
3. Inventory each obligation, condition item, maintenance episode, payment, notice, and access event with provenance and current state
4. Map supplied requirements and deadlines into owner-controlled action candidates with prerequisites, disclosure scope, and expected receipts
5. Reconcile owner-executed payments, notices, requests, appointments, and handoffs to independent same-subject receipts
6. Reconcile move-in or move-out condition, deposit or charge statements, returned property, and residual disputes without determining entitlement
7. Prepare a private handoff of deadlines, missing receipts, conflicts, safety questions, unresolved tenancy state, and exact next owners

## Example setting

**Request:** Organize my supplied apartment lease, move-in condition record, rent receipts, repair requests, entry notices, and the move-out statement. Reconcile what I already sent with independent receipts and show missing evidence and disputed charges, but do not interpret my rights, contact anyone, submit notices, pay, schedule, authorize repairs, or decide what I am owed.

**Expected outcome:** A private versioned rental ledger links the controlling supplied lease revision to obligations, condition items, maintenance episodes, owner actions, receipts, access events, move-out records, and unresolved deposit or charge questions without legal conclusions or external action.

## Standard deliverables

- Private tenancy authority, source, lease-revision, and chronology register
- Obligation, notice, deadline, payment, and independent-receipt matrix
- Premises-condition, maintenance-episode, access-event, and safety-boundary ledger
- Move-in or move-out, deposit, charge, returned-property, and dispute register
- Sensitive-data minimization and next-owner handoff

## Done when

- Every in-scope lease revision, source, obligation, notice, deadline, payment, condition item, maintenance episode, access event, action, receipt, move-state item, charge, and dispute is represented once with provenance
- Every owner-completed external action has an independent same-subject receipt or remains explicitly unreceipted
- Every missing record, conflict, safety question, disputed charge, deadline, and residual tenancy state has an accountable next owner
- The handoff makes no legal-rights, habitability, fault, liability, payment-validity, deposit-entitlement, repair-sufficiency, or closure conclusion

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
