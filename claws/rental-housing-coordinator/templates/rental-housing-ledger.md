## Source and lease revision register

List each minimized source, issuer, subject, revision, issue and retrieval time,
jurisdiction, freshness, predecessor, and successor. Attribute lease language;
do not interpret rights, duties, validity, or legal effect.

## Obligations, notices, and deadlines

For each supplied obligation or notice, record the controlling lease revision,
source, state, deadline, owner-controlled action, receipt expectation, and any
conflict. Keep contact, submission, payment, signature, and scheduling blocked.

## Premises condition and maintenance episodes

Separate renter observations, qualified findings, and landlord or manager
positions. Link every generalized condition item to its sources, maintenance
episodes, access events, charges, and open gaps. Make no habitability, fault,
safety, repair-sufficiency, or liability determination.

## Payments and independent receipts

Record only minimized period and amount notes. Reconcile each owner-reported
payment or other external action to an independent same-subject receipt. Keep
unreceipted, failed, blocked, withdrawn, and superseded actions distinct.

## Access events

Preserve the supplied notice, schedule, occurrence evidence, related
maintenance episode, renter observation, and dispute state. Do not infer
consent, lawful access, or approval.

## Move-in, move-out, returned property, and charges

Bind condition records, keys or access devices, return receipts, deposit or
charge statements, disputes, and missing evidence to their exact subjects.
Do not determine payment validity, charge entitlement, possession, or closure.

## Privacy and controlled disclosure

Use generalized premises and redacted identity, account, payment, access,
health, photo, and household references. Refuse credentials or secrets. Keep
every disclosure candidate within the recorded privacy ceiling.

## Open gaps and handoff

Index every missing record, missing receipt, conflict, safety or professional
question, disputed charge, deadline, and residual tenancy state. Name the exact
human or qualified-professional next owner and preserve all prohibited actions.
