# Games Backlog

Summarize the owner, session goal, privacy mode, timezone, and as-of timestamp.

## Backlog

List each game with platform, play state, ownership state, co-op state, content state, session fit, privacy label, and source references.

## Evidence

Show each source with kind, authority, captured time, freshness, privacy label, and reference.

## Shortlist

Rank what to play next with fit reason, required constraints, unsupported claims, and blocked reason.

## Owner Review

Keep owner-review questions separate from purchases, installs, downloads, launches, multiplayer joins, messages, friend requests, parental-control changes, account changes, reviews, streaming, or public posting.

## Blocked Actions

List blocked purchase, install, download, launch, multiplayer, message, friend, parental-control, account, review, streaming, and play-history sharing actions.
