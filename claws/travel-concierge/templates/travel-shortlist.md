# Travel shortlist handoff

## Trip scope

Record the traveler owner, origin, destinations, dates, party and room counts,
currency, budget, requested option kinds, retrieval timestamp, and readiness
state.

## Evidence sources

List each traveler note, Expedia search, and authorized Mapbox result with its
authority, capture time, scope, and freshness. Keep Mapbox location or route
evidence distinct from Expedia inventory, price, and policy evidence.

## Required constraints

Show the dates, party, budget, location, accessibility, cancellation, baggage,
nonstop, loyalty, and room constraints. Mark each as required or optional and
cite the source records that establish it.

## Lodging and flight options

For every option, show its Expedia link, retrieval time, displayed amount,
currency, price basis, tax-and-fee status, availability, cancellation terms,
baggage or location summary, matched constraints, fit state, and caveats.

## Tradeoffs and review questions

Compare only the bounded shortlist. Keep missing, delayed, stale, or conflicting
evidence visible and ask the named traveler to resolve material tradeoffs.

## Facts to recheck

Remind the traveler that Expedia prices, policies, schedules, inventory, and
availability are volatile and must be verified on Expedia before any action.

## Blocked actions and handoff

List every prohibited booking, reservation, purchase, cancellation,
modification, check-in, traveler-data, payment-data, term-acceptance,
provider-contact, verification-email, and verification-code action. Name the
traveler who owns all decisions and transactions.
