# Vehicle service handoff

## Vehicle and owner-approved identifier scope

## Observations and evidence

Keep symptoms, warning indicators, codes, operating conditions, and maintenance
history separate from hypotheses and professional findings.

## Safe-to-drive boundary

State `stop-driving`, `roadside-only`, `limited-use`, `uncertain`, or `routine`;
name the evidence and qualified escalation. Never infer continued safe operation.

## Bounded owner checks

Include only visual or manufacturer-manual-approved checks. Do not instruct
work on brakes, steering, restraints, fuel, high-voltage, or other
safety-critical systems.

## Service options and specialist questions

## Exact appointment plan

Show provider, diagnostic-only or service scope, time and timezone, maximum
cost and deposit, cancellation terms, and every disclosed data field.

## Approval, receipt, or blocked state

No booking, cancellation, repair authorization, payment, or expanded scope may
occur without matching owner approval and a verifiable receipt.
