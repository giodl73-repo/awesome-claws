# Insurance policy organizer

## Policy Ledger

List each policy with carrier, type, policy-number state, status, effective date, renewal date, evidence refs, freshness, and privacy label.

## Coverage Evidence

Separate coverage items, limits, deductibles, exclusions, source authority, and uncertainty. Do not infer coverage, eligibility, deductible applicability, claim value, compliance, or legal effect beyond supplied policy evidence.

## Premium And Renewal View

Show premium amount state, due-date state, renewal timing, notice evidence, and owner-review questions without paying premiums, changing accounts, editing calendars, or contacting anyone.

## Asset And Claim-Readiness Checklist

Bind assets and claim-readiness evidence to supplied inventories, receipts, declarations pages, endorsements, claim correspondence, carrier pages, agent notes, or owner notes. Keep gaps, stale evidence, conflicts, and redaction needs visible.

## Blocked Actions

Keep claims, coverage changes, cancellations, renewals, premium payments, carrier or agent contact, document uploads, calendar edits, address or policy-number sharing, legal advice, insurance advice, and claim-value estimates blocked until an explicit decision by the named accountable owner.
