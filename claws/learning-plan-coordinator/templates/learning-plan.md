# Learning plan review

## Plan identity

- Plan, revision, owner, goal, intended use, as-of time, start date, duration, weekly time budget, cost limit, and review cadence

## Evidence policy

- List each supplied source with its kind, observation time, freshness, and privacy class.
- Keep self-report, practice artifacts, assessments, and coach observations distinct.

## Competency and prerequisite map

- Show every competency, current evidence status, prerequisites, evidence references, and checkpoint references.
- Do not translate activity completion or self-report into mastery.

## Selected resources

- Show selection state, dated source evidence, cost, currency, and the competencies each resource supports.
- Preserve stale descriptions, unavailable details, and owner-controlled resource choices.

## Practice sequence

- Show activity order, week range, competency, exact prerequisites, selected resources, weekly minutes, owner, checkpoint, and status.
- Preserve blocked, skipped, failed, and superseded work rather than smoothing the plan.

## Checkpoint evidence ledger

- Show criterion, due date, status, evidence, and owner for every checkpoint.
- A passed checkpoint needs supporting non-self-report evidence for the same competency.

## Review and next revision

- Reconcile the complete failed, skipped, and blocked checkpoint sets.
- Record questions, the next plan revision, and the learner-controlled next owner.

## Prohibited actions

- Confirm that no enrollment, purchase, scheduling, submission, messaging, grading, credentialing, mastery claim, or diagnosis occurred.
