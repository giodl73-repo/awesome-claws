# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Creative objective, audience, format, duration, aspect ratio, visual treatment, and required variants
- Rights-cleared prompt and workspace assets, prohibited subjects, factual constraints, and disclosure requirements
- Approved PixVerse credential, endpoint region, generation budget, reviewer, and publication boundary

## Included capability boundaries

- The official PixVerse provider plugin is clean and source-linked and sends prompts and selected input media to PixVerse; configure `PIXVERSE_API_KEY` through approved host secrets outside the package and confirm region, terms, retention, model, and credit pricing.
- The minimal profile exposes only session status plus `video_generate`, with filesystem access constrained to the Claw workspace; each paid generation still requires approval of the exact prompt, inputs, settings, and expected charge.
- Generated media can contain factual, identity, rights, bias, or safety defects; retain synthetic provenance and keep outputs private until accountable human review authorizes any use.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Turn the approved brief into a generation plan with rights, safety, cost, and disclosure checks
2. Confirm the exact prompt, source assets, model settings, and expected charge before each PixVerse generation
3. Generate only the approved variants and retain prompt, settings, provider response, and output identity
4. Prepare a private review board covering visual quality, factual risk, rights, accessibility, synthetic disclosure, and revision decisions

## Example setting

**Request:** Create two six-second abstract motion concepts for an internal renewable-energy presentation using only our approved geometric brand assets.

**Expected outcome:** Two private PixVerse-generated concepts with exact prompts, settings, cost acknowledgement, synthetic provenance, rights-cleared inputs, and an editorial review board; nothing is published.

## Standard deliverables

- Approved generation brief
- Prompt and settings ledger
- Private video concept variants
- Rights, safety, and factual review checklist
- Revision or publication handoff

## Done when

- Every generation was individually approved and records prompt, settings, provider, time, output identity, and expected usage cost
- Inputs are documented as rights-cleared and contain no prohibited sensitive or deceptive subject
- Outputs remain private and visibly synthetic pending human rights, factual, brand, and accessibility review
- No publication, distribution, impersonation, or unapproved credit purchase occurred

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
