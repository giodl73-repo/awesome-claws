# Video concept generation manifest

Render `outputs/video-concept-generation-manifest.json` without converting
desired plans into observed facts. A blocked artifact may honestly contain no
outputs or incomplete review; readiness is a semantic result, not a writing
goal.

## Mode, brief, and desired result

- Label `illustrative-fixture` and `production` prominently.
- Keep the verbatim request separate from authored text.
- Show the objective, audience, private presentation use, desired two-variant
  six-second MP4 format, synthetic disclosure, owners, `asOf`, deadline, and
  readiness.
- Describe concept plans as desired plans. Never label them generated outputs.

## Structural human authority

- Show each principal's type, role, exact scopes, and attestation reference.
- For production, show the matching immutable attestation receipt.
- Planned role slots are not people and cannot approve, review, or make a
  production artifact ready.

## Pinned PixVerse and OpenClaw configuration

- Show `@openclaw/pixverse-provider@2026.7.1`, OpenClaw `v2026.7.1`,
  `pixverse/v6`, region, endpoint, reviewed terms and retention versions, and
  only the nonsecret credential reference.
- Production must show observed `video_generate` availability.
- Never render a copied credential or infer a provider account identity.

## Input plans and inspection receipts

- Keep illustrative asset intentions under planned inputs.
- Production inputs require provenance-backed inspection of existence, bytes,
  SHA-256 digest, MIME, dimensions, and 16:9 raster geometry.
- Show provenance-backed rights, permitted transformations/use/audience,
  expiry, exact human rights authority, and negative sensitive-content flags.

## Exact prompts, settings, approvals, and reservations

- Show the positive prompt, negative exclusions, exact declared abstract
  elements, and exact permitted brand vocabulary.
- Mark the image-to-video `aspectRatio` override as ignored. Bind the source
  ratio and defer output ratio truth to returned dimensions or materialization.
- For every invocation show the exact generation, cost-owner, prompt-rights,
  and prompt-safety authorities; provider region/model; terms and retention
  versions; credential reference; expected maximum; reservation; and signed or
  externally recorded approval receipt.
- Show retries only as descendants of completed failed attempts under renewed
  approval and budget.

## Provider results, billing, and materialization

- The provider receipt may show only actual exposed evidence: tool-call id,
  optional OpenClaw task id/run id and chronology, PixVerse `videoId`, terminal
  status, hosted URL, `video/mp4`, optional dimensions, normalized duration,
  and ignored overrides.
- Do not show fictional request/output ids, account identity, provider start
  time, response digest, charge, usage, or local file in the provider receipt.
- Show a separate billing receipt only when copied from the actual account
  billing system. Otherwise actual billed total is unknown and reservations
  remain conservative.
- Show a separate materialization receipt only when an approved downloader and
  probe establish local path, bytes, content digest, MIME, dimensions,
  duration, frame rate, and frame count.

## Outputs, concepts, shots, and reviews

- Cover every successful output exactly once with a same-variant concept and
  review board.
- Validate every present shot's concept, variant, output, ordinal, seconds, and
  frames. Ready output requires complete frames 0-143 with no orphan shot.
- Bind review to the hosted output identity and materialized content digest.
- List exact human editorial, rights, factual, brand, accessibility, and safety
  review receipts. Missing disciplines remain explicit and blocked.
- Review is nonfinal and cannot grant publication authority.

## Budget, controls, blockers, and handoff

- Separate expected maximum and reserved cost from observed account billing.
- Count each unreconciled attempt reservation once; replace it with a billed
  amount or release it only on a not-billed receipt.
- Resolve every proposal, question, and blocker owner and target.
- Keep proposals typed, future, and `proposed-not-executed`; scan their text
  with every other editorial string for completed actions or final approval.
- Cover every present object, policy, and authority record exactly once in the
  handoff and control bindings. Reject unused policies or principals.
- Preserve not-published, not-distributed, not-advertised, not-purchased, and
  not-authorized state.

## Illustrative-only fixture

The packaged fixture is illustrative only, blocked, and exempt from wall-clock
validation so its deterministic timestamp remains stable. It represents desired
plans, not an actual provider call, charge, output, local file, approval,
attested person, or human review.
