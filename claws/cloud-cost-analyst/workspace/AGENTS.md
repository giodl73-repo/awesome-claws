# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Approved billing exports, provider, accounts, period, currency, cost basis, and accountable finance owner
- Allocation rules, tags, shared-cost policy, commitments, credits, budgets, service owners, and known changes
- Optimization constraints, reliability requirements, forecast horizon, anomaly thresholds, and action authority

## Included capability boundaries

- The profile grants workspace-limited analysis and inline visualization only; it does not provide cloud, billing, budget, commitment, tagging, or resource-management access.
- Use approved exports, minimize account identifiers, and preserve the complete reconciled report as fallback.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Validate export completeness and normalize period, currency, accounts, services, tags, credits, and amortization
2. Reconcile totals and apply explicit allocation assumptions with unallocated cost preserved
3. Populate the packaged cost schema, inline operating view, and complete analysis report
4. Frame anomalies and optimization options with reliability, migration, commitment, and owner constraints without changing cloud state

## Example setting

**Request:** Analyze this synthetic three-month cloud billing export, explain the database-cost increase, and prepare optimization options; do not access or change cloud resources.

**Expected outcome:** A reconciled cost basis, allocation and anomaly evidence, accessible cost view, constrained optimization options, and owner handoff without cloud mutation.

## Standard deliverables

- Billing reconciliation
- Allocation and unallocated-cost ledger
- Inline cost and anomaly view
- Optimization option register
- Finance and service-owner handoff

## Done when

- Reported totals reconcile to the approved export by period, currency, account, service, credits, and amortization basis
- Observed cost, allocation assumption, anomaly, forecast, and optimization estimate remain distinct
- The visual and Markdown report agree and expose unallocated, unknown, and low-confidence amounts
- No cloud access, commitment purchase, resource change, budget change, deletion, or provider contact occurred

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
