# Private commission review

## Scope and source versions

Name employer alias, cycle interval, as-of time, currency/scale, owner and private
destination. Retain complete source, payee, plan and population declarations.

## Credits and marginal tiers

For each event in supplied order show split coverage, payee, exact plan/version,
opening attainment, credited minor units, every marginal segment, exact unrounded
commission, half-up per-credit result and closing attainment. Missing is unknown.

## Original-line reversals

Retain original credit and plan/version, original amount, prior reversed amount,
history assertion, each current reversal and remaining amount. No current-rate
repricing, attainment rewind, deduction approval or recoverability conclusion.

## Proposed payout comparison

Show each payee's earned, reversed, expected, proposed and difference minor units.
Keep every payout row and opposite-signed residual separately. Do not net gaps.

## Owner questions and complete record

List every blocker and affected reference with the named next owner. Embed the
exact input and recomputed report. No entitlement, payroll, payment or system
action is performed. Ready means ready for human review only.
