# Legal matter organizer session handoff

## Exact matter snapshot

- Record the stable matter ID, neutral title, counsel-supplied scope, snapshot
  reference, cutoff, approved workspace, controlled destination, and named
  supervising counsel.

## Preservation and evidence

- Summarize controlled sources, immutable versions, integrity digests,
  append-only supplied custody, active-hold coverage, and filing,
  correspondence, and evidence indexes without copying confidential content.

## Chronology and review state

- Distinguish occurred, reported, scheduled, and unknown events. Show deadline
  authority, timezone, verification, privilege, conflict, task, decision, and
  review-gate state with exact source references.

## Blockers and authority

- Keep malformed, missing, stale, conflicting, unsupported, inferred, or
  unconfirmed state visible. List the exact structured prohibited-action gates.

## Counsel-review handoff

- Render only `blocked` or `ready-for-counsel-review`, always with
  `not-approved`. Name the counsel reviewer and controlled destination. Never
  claim legal advice, legal action, communication, custody transfer, hold
  release, or approval.
