# Household emergency preparedness coordinator handoff

## Preparedness scope

- Record the generalized locations, declared hazards, accountable household owner, privacy ceiling, review cadence, and exact as-of time.
- State that this is pre-incident planning and name the current official alert and emergency-service paths the household must use during an incident.

## Official-source register

- Bind each hazard and generalized location to the issuing authority, controlled reference, revision, retrieval time, expiry, freshness, and known conflict.
- Never author a warning threshold, evacuation trigger, medical instruction, structural conclusion, or other live-response rule.

## Household coverage matrix

- Use privacy-safe identifiers for adults, children, dependent adults, animals, helpers, and locations.
- Reconcile each accessibility, communication, language, device-power, medication-continuity, supervision, custody, transport, and animal-care need to an owner-approved option or an explicit gap.

## Preparedness options and dependencies

- Separate owner-approved, draft, blocked, and superseded evacuation, shelter, communication, reunification, transport, animal-support, power-continuity, and document-access options.
- For each option, show applicable locations, hazards, participants, needs, source evidence, ordered plan steps, and helper, school, building, utility, transport, device, alert, or document dependencies.

## Supplies and expirations

- Preserve inventory evidence, intended participants, accountable owner, current, expired, missing, or unverified state, and exact expiry when supplied.
- Do not prescribe products, quantities, treatment, survival methods, or specialist work.

## Drills, findings, and corrections

- Bind each exercise to its scope, represented participants, observed result, owner-controlled evidence, findings, corrective actions, due dates, and completion receipts or accepted gaps.

## Owner review and blocked authority

- List every uncovered need, unconfirmed or unavailable dependency, expired or missing supply, open finding, and open corrective action before naming the next revision and owner.
- Confirm that no incident was monitored, alert issued, threshold interpreted, evacuation declared, emergency direction given, person contacted, registration submitted, purchase made, transport booked, location published, household data transmitted, professional advice given, or readiness certified.
- Leave the private packet at `outputs/household-emergency-preparedness-coordinator-handoff.md` for the accountable household owner's review.
