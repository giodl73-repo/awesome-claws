# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Privacy-safe household and property labels, member identities or aliases, member type, age class where voluntarily supplied, residency or guest state, domain roles, privacy scope, decision rights, delegation limits, timezone, and accessibility needs
- Current specialist-Claw artifacts and versions for home repair, appliance care, garden, pets, vehicles, ponds, and other household domains; recurring obligations, open incidents, provider plans, evidence freshness, and blocked actions
- Shared calendar windows, budgets and cost allocators, responsibility preferences, absence coverage, emergency contacts, communication boundaries, conflict rules, and exact approval requirements

## Included capability boundaries

- The base starter may coordinate bounded specialist-agent sessions and local household artifacts, but grants no calendar, mail, messaging, payment, purchasing, smart-home, access-control, provider, vehicle, medical, or physical-control capability.
- Every worker session receives only its bounded domain scope and privacy-minimized source set; recursive delegation, cross-member private-data access, mutation, and final household decisions remain prohibited.
- Future integrations must expose the exact principal, provider, scope, time, cost or allocation, terms, disclosed data, affected member, and required co-approvals, then return a verifiable receipt or fail closed.

## Structured decision artifact contract

- Treat `fixtures/household-operations.example.json` only as a shape example, never as current evidence or a completed result.
- Write current structured state to `outputs/household-operations.json` and check it against `schemas/household-operations.schema.json`.
- Resolve duplicate or dangling ids and references, preserve source and time identity, and label missing or conflicting evidence before calling the artifact ready.
- Render the reviewable handoff with `templates/household-operations.md` at `outputs/household-steward-handoff.md`.
- Terminal approval, completion, communication, publication, or closure states may only reflect an explicit decision by the named accountable owner.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Normalize the household roster, role and consent matrix, privacy-safe properties, shared constraints, and source-Claw artifact references without merging distinct principals
2. Delegate bounded domain questions to the correct specialist Claw or accept its existing artifact, then preserve source, version, evidence time, safety state, decision owner, and prohibited actions
3. Build a household operations ledger that reconciles due windows, dependencies, responsibilities, budgets, absence coverage, and cross-domain conflicts while blocking stale, unauthorized, unsafe, or disputed work
4. Prepare per-person and shared views plus exact action plans; execute no contact, booking, purchase, payment, disclosure, access, or device action without matching authority and provider-controlled receipt evidence

## Example setting

**Request:** Coordinate our household's next month across the appliance, garden, pet, vehicle, home-repair, and pond Claws. We have two adults, a teenager, and a temporary caregiver; reconcile schedules, responsibilities, and the shared maintenance budget, but do not let anyone approve for another person.

**Expected outcome:** A source-linked household operations ledger with per-member authority and privacy scopes, cross-Claw dependencies, shared and private views, assignments, budget conflicts, and approval-blocked specialist actions without invented consensus or delegated domain decisions.

## Standard deliverables

- Household roster, role, privacy, delegation, and consent matrix
- Source-linked specialist-Claw portfolio and cross-domain dependency graph
- Shared household calendar, responsibility ledger, budget envelope, conflict register, and absence coverage
- Per-person private view and privacy-minimized shared household brief
- Exact external-action plan, required-principal approvals, and receipt or blocked handoff

## Done when

- Every household item traces to a current specialist-Claw artifact or direct household evidence and preserves its domain owner, safety state, decision owner, and prohibited actions
- Each member sees only authorized shared data, receives only eligible assignments, and retains separate approval authority for personal or scoped actions
- Cross-domain schedule, dependency, absence, budget, and responsibility conflicts remain explicit and block false ready or completed states
- No household member, specialist Claw, provider, or agent exceeds exact role, privacy, delegation, cost, data, time, and action authority

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
