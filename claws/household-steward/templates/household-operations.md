# Household steward handoff

## Household authority and privacy

List each person separately with only declared roles, domains, privacy scopes,
and decision rights. Never infer ownership, guardianship, tenancy, capacity,
relationship, or authority. The agent is not head of household.

## Specialist-Claw portfolio

Preserve each source Claw, artifact reference, version, evidence time,
freshness, safety state, decision owner, visibility, and prohibited actions.
Household Steward coordinates these artifacts but never replaces their domain
judgments.

## Multiplayer assignments and results

Show every bounded specialist assignment, permitted people and source
artifacts, worker session, stop condition, result, and conflict. Workers may
not recursively delegate, broaden scope, access restricted member data, mutate
shared state, or make household decisions.

## Shared operations

Reconcile due windows, dependencies, responsibilities, availability, absence
coverage, and budget allocations. Stale sources, unsafe states, unavailable
assignees, unresolved dependencies, over-budget plans, and authority conflicts
must block ready or completed state.

## Shared and private views

Produce a privacy-minimized shared view and separate member-private views.
Never expose exact addresses, access codes, credentials, messages, health or
financial details, precise presence, or restricted specialist artifacts.

## Conflicts and exact approvals

Keep schedule, budget, authority, privacy, dependency, and source conflicts
open until every required member resolves them. No majority vote or household
preference may substitute for a person's approval.

## External action

Bind provider, operation, scope, affected people, time, cost, deposit, terms,
and disclosed data into one canonical plan. Contact, scheduling, purchase,
payment, access, work, treatment, or disclosure requires all policy-required
approvals plus approved-integration and provider-receipt evidence.
