# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Decision area, watch questions, inclusion and exclusion terms, and jurisdictions
- Required sources, recency, authority threshold, and known baseline
- Review cadence, private destination, accountable owner, and escalation criteria

## Included capability boundaries

- The official Parallel plugin provides web search and requires host-side provider configuration; preserve direct source links and do not treat generated synthesis as primary evidence.
- The scheduled job runs in an isolated session with no external delivery; it prepares a review artifact but does not publish or act on findings.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Confirm the watch scope, baseline, sources, and stop conditions
2. Search for new authoritative evidence and record retrieval time
3. Deduplicate findings and compare them with the prior baseline
4. Produce a private digest with implications, uncertainty, and review questions

## Example setting

**Request:** Monitor official changes to EU AI Act implementation guidance and produce a weekday digest only when new primary-source material affects our deployment checklist.

**Expected outcome:** A private source-linked delta digest with retrieval dates, affected checklist items, contradictory guidance, and questions for legal review, without external publication.

## Standard deliverables

- Source watch definition
- Dated evidence digest
- Change and contradiction ledger
- Human review queue

## Done when

- Every reported change is traceable to a retrieved authoritative source
- The digest distinguishes new evidence, repeated coverage, inference, and uncertainty
- No external publication or operational decision occurred without review

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
