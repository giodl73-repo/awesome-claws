# Web claim-evidence investigation handoff

Render this template only from an artifact that validates against
`schemas/claim-evidence-investigation-ledger.schema.json`. Keep the ledger and
rendered report private at the declared workspace destination.

## Bounded investigation control

- Investigation and run IDs:
- Private research question and supported decision:
- Named human or team decision owner:
- Included scope and explicit exclusions:
- Private classification, output destination, run timing, and as-of time:
- State: draft / blocked / ready:

This is a bounded claim-oriented web investigation, not a recurring source
watch, a feed-triage workflow, a scholarly evidence lifecycle, or a raw website
capture. Show every owner-approved public authority, domain, permitted source
type, query, execution time, and returned source record. An empty result list is
an explicit zero-result search; it does not authorize a broader search, another
service, or an unapproved authority.

## Public source and query provenance

For every retained source, show its canonical key and URL, approved authority,
publisher, source type, query records, publication, update, and retrieval
times, freshness, recheck state, independence key, derivation state, and source
scope. Use only credential-free public HTTPS URLs on owner-approved domains.

Each retained source must identify the query records that returned it, and every
query must link back to its results. Do not use a mirror, syndicated copy, or
derived page as independent corroboration. Preserve the source identity rather
than copying restricted content, credentials, sensitive query text, fragments,
or private-network references.

## Hypotheses, claims, and typed evidence

For every hypothesis, show its decomposed claim records and status. For each
claim, render the exact bounded statement, claim type, evidence links,
support/refute/context/unknown assessment, independent-corroboration rule,
decision implication, confidence, uncertainty, limitations, and resolution
status.

For every evidence record, show its source, type, stance, short direct quote or
bounded excerpt, confidence, and limitation. An excerpt is evidence of the
named source only; it must not be presented as a consensus, causal finding,
legal, medical, financial, or autonomous decision inference.

## Conflicts, owner questions, and gaps

Show every conflict or contradiction with its claim and evidence links, owner,
status, and resolution. Show every owner review question, unresolved question,
gap, or blocker explicitly. Do not convert absent evidence, a zero-result
search, matching language, source counts, rankings, mirrors, or vendor
documentation into a conclusion.

## Owner review and private handoff

Repeat all source, hypothesis, claim, evidence, conflict, question, gap,
blocker, and owner-review references. A ready handoff requires current
rechecked sources, classified evidence, resolved claims/questions/gaps, a
completed human owner review, and no open blocker. It is ready only for the
named owner's review, never for an autonomous decision or action.

## Authority gates

Do not bypass access controls, reproduce restricted content, publish or contact
externally, subscribe or change accounts, disclose credentials or sensitive
queries, fabricate sources, quotes, or claims, infer consensus, causality,
legal, medical, or financial conclusions, or change decisions or actions
autonomously. Those actions remain with authorized people outside this artifact.
