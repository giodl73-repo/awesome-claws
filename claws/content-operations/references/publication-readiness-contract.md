# Publication readiness contract

Content Operations owns the reviewable state between an editorial brief and a
human-controlled publication action. It does not own the source documents, the
draft text, a CMS, distribution lists, or analytics results.

The record keeps the request, audience, intended action, acceptance criteria,
sources, claims, exact asset versions, reviews, measurement definitions, gaps,
and handoff in one graph. Claims are usable only when their sources are current
and appropriate for the claim kind. Asset readiness is derived from claim and
criterion coverage rather than asserted in prose.

Approval is scoped. A reviewer approves an exact asset version and named
claims; a later version must be reviewed again. Publication readiness requires
all approval kinds requested by the included claims plus a channel-owner
approval. It never performs publication.

Measurement remains a handoff definition until the owner-controlled analytics
system produces observations. The Claw must not convert a target or success
measure into a claimed result.

Blocked records are valid deliverables. They enumerate every unresolved item
and preserve drafts without manufacturing evidence or approval.
