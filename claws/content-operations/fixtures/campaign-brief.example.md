# FlowDesk campaign brief

Private draft v1 for Campaign lead. Internal review only.

## Objective and audience

Invite operations managers responsible for procurement intake to request a
FlowDesk demonstration. The proposed campaign introduces configurable request
forms and sequential approval steps; it does not promise savings.

## Message and creative direction

Bring procurement requests into a configurable form and route them through
sequential approval steps.

Use a product walkthrough showing a request form and its approval sequence.
This is a proposed creative treatment, not a supplied image or completed
design asset. Product lead must check any future screenshots against version
2.4; Brand team reviews the treatment.

## Deliverables and dependencies

Email v1: a concise invitation, with the final demo destination still pending.
Web v1: a paragraph describing the same supported workflow.
Marketing team owns destination, eligible audience and channel decisions.
Design work and any asset production remain owner assignments, not completed
deliverables. No spend or schedule is set.

## Measurement proposal

Proposed rate: unique eligible delivered-email recipients completing a valid
demo request within seven days of first delivery, divided by unique eligible
delivered-email recipients. Deduplicate recipients and requests; exclude tests,
internal recipients, suppressed addresses and bounces. A zero denominator is
undefined. Unmatched event identities are unknown and reported separately.

Analytics team owns the delivery-event and demo-request-event join, UTC
timestamps, permitted identifiers, exclusions and window maturity. Marketing
and Analytics must agree the seven-day window before use. No event data,
conversion result or uplift estimate is supplied.

## Evidence and review handoff

Forms and sequential approvals come from the permitted FlowDesk 2.4 product
brief. Email and web copy use only those two capability claims. Quantified
savings and the customer quote are withheld for lack of evidence or permission;
their absence does not prevent review of supported copy.

Factual, brand, legal and channel-owner decisions are pending for each v1 asset.
Campaign lead receives the drafts and publication-readiness record. Exact
approvals must be revisited after any edit. Nothing is published, sent,
scheduled, spent or activated.
