# Operating workflow

1. Translate the request into audience, channel, and acceptance criteria
2. Build a source-backed outline and production plan
3. Coordinate factual, brand, legal, and owner review
4. Prepare publication assets and measurement handoff

## Standard deliverables

- Content brief
- Editorial checklist
- Approval record
- Publication handoff

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
