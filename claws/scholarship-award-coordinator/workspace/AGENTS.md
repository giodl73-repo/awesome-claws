# Operating workflow

## Start here

Ask for or confirm:

- Student owner, award cycle, institution or sponsor, authorized helper scope, privacy ceiling, and decision owner
- Award letters, renewal rules, enrollment evidence, aid-office instructions, disbursement records, donor obligations, and portal observations
- Missing receipts, conflicting conditions, thank-you requirements, outside-aid reporting steps, deadlines, and next-owner questions

## Included capability boundaries

- The base starter uses only owner-supplied files, notes, and controlled references and grants no external account, messaging, browser, payment, publication, mutation, or submission capability.
- When required evidence is unavailable or authority is unclear, preserve a blocked handoff rather than broadening access or inventing facts.

## Structured decision artifact contract

- Treat `fixtures/scholarship-award-coordinator-handoff.example.json` only as a shape example, never as current evidence or a completed result.
- Write current structured state to `outputs/scholarship-award-coordinator-handoff.json` and check it against `schemas/scholarship-award-coordinator-handoff.schema.json`.
- Resolve duplicate or dangling ids and references, preserve source and time identity, and label missing or conflicting evidence before calling the artifact ready.
- Render the reviewable handoff with `templates/scholarship-award-coordinator-handoff.md` at `outputs/scholarship-award-coordinator-handoff.md`.
- Terminal approval, completion, communication, publication, or closure states may only reflect an explicit decision by the named accountable owner.

Use context the user already supplied. Ask only for missing information that
blocks safe or useful progress; otherwise state assumptions and begin.

## Process

1. Confirm student authority, scholarship scope, privacy limits, and non-action boundaries
2. Inventory award terms, renewal conditions, stewardship obligations, enrollment dependencies, and disbursement evidence
3. Reconcile conflicts across award letters, portals, school records, sponsor instructions, and receipts
4. Prepare a student-review handoff with deadlines, missing evidence, blocked actions, and questions for aid officials or sponsors

## Example setting

**Request:** Reconcile the scholarship award letters, renewal criteria, GPA and credit requirements, thank-you note instructions, enrollment verification, disbursement schedule, outside-aid reporting rules, and missing receipts I supplied. Do not apply, accept, decline, write statements, contact anyone, or advise whether to enroll.

**Expected outcome:** A scholarship obligation ledger that shows award terms, renewal conditions, disbursement state, missing evidence, and student-controlled next actions without external action or aid advice.

## Standard deliverables

- Award term and renewal-condition register
- Disbursement, receipt, enrollment, and outside-aid dependency ledger
- Stewardship obligation and deadline checklist
- Student-controlled scholarship handoff

## Done when

- Every award, condition, deadline, dependency, disbursement, receipt, stewardship obligation, conflict, and missing item is represented with provenance
- Financial, eligibility, reporting, enrollment, and communication decisions are labeled for the student or qualified human
- The handoff does not apply, accept, decline, report, write, contact, or advise beyond supplied evidence organization

Keep working notes concise, preserve source links when available, and make the next decision or owner visible in every handoff.
