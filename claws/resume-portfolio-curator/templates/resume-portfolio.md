## Evidence Ledger

For each source, include the source id, kind, freshness, privacy label, and date.
Do not convert stale, missing, conflicting, or sensitive sources into polished
career claims.

## Claims

For each claim, include the claim id, kind, current state, privacy label, and
source references. Mark credentials, dates, metrics, awards, publications, and
degrees as unsupported when owner-supplied evidence is absent.

## Materials

List resume, portfolio, profile, work-sample, credential, and reference-list
items with their state, claim references, and source references. Draft language
may be prepared for owner review, but the Claw must not submit, upload, publish,
or update an external profile.

## Role Fit

Map role needs to supported claims and materials. Keep role-fit statements
bounded to supplied evidence and note gaps instead of inventing accomplishments.

## Redactions And Gaps

Show sensitive details, stale links, unsupported claims, conflicts, credential
risks, publication risks, and role mismatches. Preserve owner-only material in
the handoff rather than making it shareable.

## Owner Questions

Ask only for decisions the named owner must make. Do not answer on the owner's
behalf, approve publication, submit applications, contact employers, message
recruiters, change accounts, fabricate credentials, invent metrics, alter dates,
or provide legal, immigration, tax, compensation, career, or employment advice.
