# Home Inventory Binder

Summarize the owner, purpose, privacy mode, timezone, and as-of timestamp.

## Inventory

List each item with room, category, condition, value state, privacy label, inventory state, blocked reason, and source references.

## Evidence

Show each receipt, photo, serial, manual, purchase record, value note, condition note, and warranty source with authority, freshness, privacy, and reference.

## Warranties

Show warranty state, expiration evidence, missing evidence, unsupported warranty claims, and owner-review questions.

## Privacy Review

Call out address-sensitive rooms, valuable-sensitive items, serial numbers, photos, receipts, family details, and any details that should stay redacted.

## Blocked Actions

List blocked claim, insurance-advice, legal-advice, upload, public-share, insurer-contact, seller-contact, sale, donation, disposal, move, cloud-album edit, address-disclosure, and valuables-disclosure actions.
