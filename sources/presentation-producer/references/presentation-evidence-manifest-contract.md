# Presentation evidence manifest contract

The review-copy PPTX is the primary deliverable. Presentation Producer owns a
durable evidence and review sidecar for one exact deck path, digest, and version.
The manifest does not duplicate PowerPoint object state or presentation-tool
runtime state. It retains only the structured extracted title/material-text
items required for content/claim coverage and canonical review evidence.

The contract freezes the bounded request, provenance-backed authority registry,
authorized immutable inputs, structured source-use relevance and freshness
assessments, an exact extracted template inventory, source and template
preservation rules, exact 12-slide inventory, per-slide extracted-content claim
coverage, material claims, citations, approved speaker-note content, visual
provenance, render identities, visual QA, content extraction QA, scoped review
approval, unresolved work, and private handoff.

Every material claim remains epistemically typed. Final factual claims require
current, authorized, exact-version source evidence. Assumptions and inferences
stay visibly labeled. Decisions and recommendations remain owned by named humans
or teams even when the exact review copy has passed evidence and visual review.

Review approval is not distribution authority. A deterministic canonical
content digest binds one exact review-copy identity, every source-use
assessment, complete material claim, slide title and extracted content item,
visual, citation, note, render set and record, QA finding and configuration,
control, blocker, and handoff field. Final content QA has its own canonical
digest. Any changed claim, title, extracted-text digest, render digest, or
unregistered material title/body content stales approval.

Every title and material body item appears in the per-slide extracted-content
map and resolves to the slide's exact material claim set. The canonical
per-slide and full-deck extracted-text digests prevent content from being added
outside that map.

Visual QA is evidence, not a declaration. The first render set is itself a
complete failing full-deck render with an observed finding on the exact failed
slide render. Its fix time follows discovery and precedes the later fixed
render and later full-deck rerender. Every later cycle also covers every slide and checks overflow, clipping,
contrast, placeholders, citation collisions, overlap, edge margin, and notes
leakage. A passed final render cannot contain a failed check or open finding.

Control inheritance follows actual slide references transitively and is
cycle-safe: slides reach their claims, citations, notes, visuals, assessments,
assets, and sources. The review copy and top-level manifest exactly equal the
strongest classification, audience intersection, complete license set, every
retention policy, and longest retention date from that full deck-content
closure. A public manifest therefore cannot front confidential content.

Each actual source, claim-evidence, template, source-deck, and visual use has a
human-reviewed assessment bound to its exact source version, digest, use,
observed time, dated freshness threshold, manifest as-of boundary, and
registered reviewer. A prose relevance rationale is explanatory only.

Template fidelity is measured against a canonical extracted inventory digest
and version. Preservation lists and every slide master/layout must resolve to
that inventory. The exact template ratio is preserved; both 16:9 and 4:3 are
valid when the inventory proves them.

Visuals record an exact source locator and visible-content provenance. Notes,
comments, and hidden content are excluded, and approved visual audiences cannot
be broader than either the source or deck closure. All authored strings,
including alt text and assessment rationale, are scanned for unsupported action
or leakage claims. Owner actions are represented only as structured proposals;
proposal-like prose receives no clause-wide exemption.

Blocked and pre-review manifests are valid deliverables and may have no approval
records or handoff approval references. A missed deadline produces one exact
deadline blocker and a blocked handoff rather than invalidating an otherwise
coherent artifact. Manifest `asOf` is the deterministic validation boundary for
all observed, render, QA, review, and approval chronology; validation never
uses the machine clock, so an internally coherent future-dated illustrative
artifact is not rejected merely for being in the future.

Content QA always scans the standard PowerPoint placeholder baseline (`xxxx`,
`lorem`, `ipsum`, `this page layout`, and `this slide layout`) plus every
template-derived placeholder pattern. An arbitrary sentinel alone is not
acceptable proof.

Distribution, publication, sending, external upload, source overwrite,
hidden-content exposure, and approval or decision authority remain prohibited.
The complete handoff stays private, local, and not delivered.

The packaged example is illustrative manifest evidence only. It does not include
or claim to have created a real PPTX, source deck, template deck, render, or
review approval.
