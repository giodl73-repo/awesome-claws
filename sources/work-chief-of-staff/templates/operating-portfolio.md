# Work chief of staff operating portfolio

## Portfolio and principals

- Record the planning horizon, as-of time, each human principal, declared role,
  decision scopes, confidentiality scopes, delegation limits, and authority
  evidence. Do not infer hierarchy or authority.

## Source portfolio

- Preserve each specialist Claw id, artifact reference and version, freshness,
  accountable owner, decision owner, native status meaning, confidentiality,
  permitted audience, and prohibited actions.

## Bounded worker results

- Record the exact assignment, privacy-minimized sources, worker session,
  result, stop condition, and unresolved disagreement. Workers do not mutate
  source systems or make commitments.

## Outcomes, dependencies, and capacity

- Show each workstream's owner, decision owner, outcome, priority, dates,
  capacity demand, dependencies, decision forum, and blocked reasons.
- Keep over-allocation and incompatible priority, timing, authority, source, or
  confidentiality states visible as conflicts.

## Decision forums and audience views

- Prepare only the material authorized for each leadership, functional, or
  forum audience. Exclude personnel, compensation, customer, legal, security,
  finance, roadmap, acquisition, credentials, and restricted source detail
  unless every source permits that audience.

## Commitment gate

- Canonicalize the exact commitment plan and require every policy-named
  principal to approve its SHA-256 digest.
- A completed commitment also requires an approved integration and
  controlled-system receipt bound to that digest. Otherwise remain blocked.

## Handoff

- Name unresolved decisions and accountable principals.
- Never act as an executive, infer decision rights, override leader conflict,
  replace specialist judgment, expose restricted data, authorize for another
  principal, perform a commitment, or expand worker scope.
