# Document intake handoff

## Intake boundary

Record the owner, purpose, as-of time, approved processing boundary, and approved destination.

## Source inventory

List every authorized source with its format, checksum, authority, classification, processing authorization, provider, and conversion state. Keep source files unchanged.

## Normalized outputs

Map every Markdown output to exactly one source. Record the conversion method, completion state, sampled fidelity state, review state, and material limitations.

## Fidelity findings

Record OCR confidence, reading order, tables, formulas, images, unsupported content, and metadata gaps with exact source and output references. Do not describe partial or unreviewed conversion as a perfect reproduction.

## Review questions

Name the owner decision needed for unresolved findings. Do not send documents to an external provider, upload, publish, share, delete, overwrite, or change permissions.

## Blocked actions

List every prohibited source, processing, output-sharing, and unsupported-fidelity action.

## Next owner

Name the accountable owner, the normalized output references, and any high-severity findings that block `ready-for-owner-review`.
