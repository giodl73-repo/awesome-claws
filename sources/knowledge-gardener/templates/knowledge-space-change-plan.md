# Knowledge-space change plan

## Plan boundary

Render the plan id, verbatim request, as-of time, review horizon, status,
maintenance owner, stale-decision threshold, observation goals, conventions,
and the operator-supplied local export's noncredential id, integration
registration id, positive version, digest, export and supply times,
`containsSecrets: false`, matching authorization/scope receipt, exact shared
roots, databases, pages, and excluded object ids.

## Observed Notion snapshots

Render every globally unique stable object id and canonical URL identity, type,
credential-free reference, exact last-edited version, parent and root ancestry,
operator observation and export inclusion chronology, source owner, authority,
access, classification, audience, retention, exact scope and exclusion proof,
observed patch values, and missing links or properties.

## Detected issues

List duplicate topics, stale decisions, conflicts, broken or missing links,
missing properties, and orphan candidates. Mark each as observed or inferred,
cite exact-version snapshots, keep related links reciprocal, and preserve every
conflict side without resolution.

## Proposed reversible operations

For each link, property update, rename, page draft, move, or archive proposal,
show its exact target version, before and after value, patch intent, affected
objects, exact issue and source evidence closure, proposal time after all
evidence, impact, dependencies, blockers, expected access and handling controls,
rollback patch, state, and external-human application mode. Never render an
executed or applied state, and never approve an operation touching an unresolved
conflict side.

## Exact-version approvals

Render only operation-specific human or team approvals bound to the current
plan digest and all affected exact observed versions. Mark version drift,
target drift, plan drift, early evidence, missing ownership, or blanket scope as
invalid rather than implying approval.

## Blockers and questions

Render every dependency, blocker, and owner question with reciprocal targets.
A ready-for-human-review plan has no open blocker or blocking question.

## Complete private handoff

Cover every snapshot, issue, operation, approval, blocker, and question exactly
once. Preserve transitive classification, audience, and retention through
cycle-safe references. Keep the JSON plan and Markdown handoff private, local,
and not delivered.

## Prohibited authority

Keep out-of-scope reads; Notion create, update, archive, move, delete, publish,
share, and access-control changes; restricted-content copying; autonomous
conflict, decision, or retention action; and claims of access, mutation, or
completion explicitly prohibited. Use no network, exec, Notion API, or Notion
skill. Application always remains external and human-controlled.
