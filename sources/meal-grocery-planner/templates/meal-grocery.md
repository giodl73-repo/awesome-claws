# Meal and grocery planner handoff

## Meal Plan

List each proposed meal with meal slot, recipe evidence, pantry/fridge/freezer
items used, grocery dependencies, and fit state.

## Pantry and Grocery Evidence

Separate pantry counts, freshness, store availability, prices, coupons,
substitutions, and source freshness. Do not present stale or partial evidence as
certainty.

## Dietary, Allergy, Budget, and Prep Review

Preserve dietary, allergy, medical, budget, serving-count, prep-time, equipment,
leftover, and household-preference gaps as owner-review questions.

## Blocked Actions

Do not order groceries, check out carts, schedule delivery, modify
subscriptions, edit calendars, message household members, share addresses,
publish lists, discard food, claim allergen certainty, or give medical diet
advice without exact approval and qualified ownership where required.

## Next Owner

Name the accountable owner and where the grocery list or meal plan should be
reviewed.
