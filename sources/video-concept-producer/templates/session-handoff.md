# Video concept producer handoff

Validate `outputs/video-concept-generation-manifest.json` against
`schemas/video-concept-generation-manifest.schema.json`, then render it with
`templates/video-concept-generation-manifest.md`. Do not render fixture values as
current evidence.

## Brief, mode, and structural authority

- Label illustrative and production mode, show the two desired plans, and keep
  plans distinct from observed outputs.
- Show the bounded objective, private audience and use, desired format,
  synthetic disclosure, prohibited subjects, publication boundary, authority
  roles, as-of time, deadline, readiness, and manifest digest.
- Production authority must link typed human roles and exact scopes to immutable
  attestations.

## Provider, inspected inputs, approvals, and exact calls

- Show the nonsecret credential reference, region, endpoint, terms/retention
  versions, `pixverse/v6`, plugin `2026.7.1`, OpenClaw `v2026.7.1`, currency,
  cap, and observed `video_generate` availability for production.
- Show each input's provenance-backed existence, bytes, digest, MIME,
  dimensions, 16:9 geometry, rights, safety flags, and permitted vocabulary.
- Show the exact prompt, negative exclusions, ignored image-to-video
  aspect-ratio override, settings, expected maximum, reservation, exact
  generation/cost/rights/safety actors, immutable approval receipt, request
  digest, chronology, failure, and retry lineage.

## Provider, billing, materialization, and output truth

- Limit provider results to exposed tool/task identity, PixVerse `videoId`,
  terminal status, hosted URL, MIME, optional dimensions, normalization, and
  chronology.
- Show actual charge or usage only from a separate account billing receipt.
  Show a local path, content digest, dimensions, duration, or frames only from a
  separate materialization and media-probe receipt.
- Cover each successful output exactly once with a same-variant concept and
  review board. Validate every present shot; ready state needs complete frames
  0-143 and all six provenance-backed human review disciplines.

## Nonpublication and next owner

- Resolve proposal, question, and blocker owners and targets; keep proposals
  typed, future, and not executed.
- Cover every object, policy, and principal exactly once. Complete coverage does
  not make incomplete evidence ready.
- Keep publication `not-published`, distribution `not-distributed`, advertising
  `not-advertised`, credit purchase `not-purchased`, and publication authority
  `not-granted`.
