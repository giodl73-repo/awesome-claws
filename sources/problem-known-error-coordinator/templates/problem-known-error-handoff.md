# Problem and known-error owner review

## Problem revision and predecessor

Record the exact open problem revision, predecessor artifact/revision digests,
service scope, owner declaration, and caller cutoff.

## Incident membership manifest

List exactly three owner-signed memberships. Preserve each complete Incident
Response artifact and schema digest, incident revision, follow-up identity,
source bytes, declaration chronology, and independent receipt.

## Hypothesis, QA, and disposition matrix

Keep proposal and disposition revisions separate. Bind each test to its exact
QA artifact, run, build, environment, executor, evidence, and strictly later
receipt. Tests cannot predate or equal proposal time; disposition cannot
predate or equal any consumed execution or evidence.

## Workaround and known error

Show the unexpired owner-approved workaround without approving, publishing, or
executing it. Show the owner-declared known-error revision without inferring
root cause or publication.

## Change finalization and recurrence

Bind the external owner-executed change to the complete Change Control artifact,
schema, and plan. Recurrence must follow finalized execution, verification,
problem linkage, source observation, and later incident membership.

## Coverage and trust

Show exact closed coverage, caller-supplied keyring and issuer allowlist,
verified-human credentials, grants, evidence claims, source attestations, and
principal-signed owner receipts. Reject omitted, duplicated, stale, unsigned,
unresolved, equal-time, or oversized inputs.

## Authority

Correlation, root cause, workaround approval/execution, known-error
publication, production change, ticket mutation, closure, and risk acceptance
remain with existing owners.
