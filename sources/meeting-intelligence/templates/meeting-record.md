# Meeting record handoff

## Meeting, authority, and retention

Record the meeting and run identity, purpose, named human decision owner and
reviewer, start and end time with an explicit timezone, authorized audience and
scope, privacy level, private output destination, retention policy, deletion
date, retention owner, and whether the record is a draft, blocked, or ready for
owner review. State the recording authority, the consent basis, the notice time,
and the local uses that were authorized. Recording authority, every required
local use, and local-only processing hold in every state, not just at readiness.
A record built on restricted material stays inside a named audience rather than
the whole organization, and the private destination never overwrites the
generated document.

## Source recording and evidence register

Identify exactly one source recording with its custodian, capture time,
workspace-relative path, and content digest. List the recording authority,
consent register, retention policy, participant roster, document template,
acknowledgement records, correction notes, and owner notes with their integrity
and privacy. Record the processed window, language, channel, and audio quality,
and confirm that transcription stayed local. The recording is captured inside
the meeting it records, the processed window never claims more audio than the
meeting lasted, and acknowledgement, correction, and owner evidence cannot
predate the meeting or the passage it supports.

## Participants and consent

List every participant with their role, organization, attendance, speaker label,
consent status, consent basis, consent scopes, consent time, consent evidence,
and withdrawal time. Consent is never inferred from attendance, silence, role,
or a majority. Speech from a participant without transcription consent stays
withheld, and withdrawn or missing consent is carried as an explicit gap.
Granted consent covers recording, transcription, attributed quotation, internal
minutes, and action assignment; limited consent names the scopes it leaves out.

## Transcript

For each segment record the exact start and end offsets, the speaker attribution
state and confidence, the uncertainty marker, and whether the excerpt is
verbatim. Keep excerpts short: the transcript is sensitive evidence, not the
deliverable. A withheld segment keeps its offsets and its withheld reason, and
its excerpt is only a quotation-free `[Withheld: ...]` marker; the speech itself
never survives redaction. Corrections must link the corrected segment to the
segment it supersedes through a named human and a verified correction note.
Never invent a speaker, a quotation, or a passage the audio does not support.

## Deliberations, decisions, and dissent

Keep proposals, discussion, and information separate from decisions. Each
decision names an attending chair or decision maker, an explicit authority
basis, an explicit agreement basis, its supporting segments, its status, its
conditions, its review state, and any recorded dissent with the conflict it
belongs to. Agreement is never inferred from silence or an unprompted absence of
objection. Crediting someone with raising a topic, dissenting, or taking part in
a conflict claims they spoke on the record, so it requires an attending
participant with a speaker label, transcription consent, and, for a raiser, a
cited segment attributed to them. A proposal whose speaker could not be
identified leaves the raiser unnamed rather than guessing.

## Action items

Each action cites its transcript provenance, distinguishes an acknowledged
assignment from a proposed one, names its due date with an explicit timezone,
records its status and dependencies, and leaves messages, calendar changes, and
task-system writes to the named owner. A date-only due date means the end of
that day in the timezone it names, not in UTC. An owner-ready record carries no
action without a named assignee: work nobody will own belongs in the open
questions or the parking lot until a named human takes it. An assignment that
its named assignee has not yet acknowledged stays proposed and goes to the owner
for confirmation.

## Questions, parking lot, conflicts, and gaps

Preserve unresolved questions with their owner, parked topics with the
deliberation that produced them, conflicts with their resolution state, and
every gap: inaudible audio, unusable recordings, missing or withdrawn consent,
unknown speakers, untranscribed windows, and missing templates or authority.
Every unattributed passage is carried by an unknown-speaker gap, degraded or
unusable audio is named as its own gap, and stretches of the processed window
the transcript does not cover are declared as untranscribed rather than left
implied. A follow-up owner needs consent to action assignment but need not have
attended. A blocked handoff names real unresolved work, which may be an open
question, an unresolved conflict, or a document that was never created, instead
of an invented blocking gap. A meeting with no decisions and no actions is
recorded as exactly that.

## Document draft, blocked actions, and owner review

The generated document is a review draft that preserves the original template
and the source recording, stays bound to the authorized audience, and carries
every decision and action. List the prohibited recording, inference,
attribution, fabrication, commitment, communication, scheduling, task-creation,
biometric, disclosure, retention, deletion, and publication actions. Name the
accountable owner and reviewer, include every deliberation, decision, action,
question, parked topic, conflict, correction, and gap in the handoff, and keep
each blocking gap visible until the named human resolves it.
