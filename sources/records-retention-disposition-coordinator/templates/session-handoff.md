# Records retention and disposition coordinator handoff

## Exact inventory snapshot

- Record the stable snapshot ID, cutoff, timezone, approved workspace and
  destination, inventory-completeness state, and every named human authority
  scope.

## Schedule, records, copies, and eligibility

- Show the authoritative schedule key, version, digest, effective period,
  series, records, every known copy and location, supplied classification,
  trigger evidence, matching rule, and exact timezone-safe eligibility
  calculation. Preserve all forward and reverse lineage.

## Holds, exceptions, batch, and approval

- Render legal, regulatory, and audit hold scope before eligibility. Active,
  unknown, conflicting, incomplete, or broader holds block readiness and
  completion. Show exception request and independent approval evidence,
  expiry, proposal items, deterministic batch digest, and independent
  post-evidence approval chronology.

## Custody, outcomes, certificates, and residuals

- Keep append-only custody separate from disposition outcomes. Show requested,
  attempted, failed, partial, succeeded, and unknown state exactly. A request
  or attempt is not execution evidence. Completion needs an authoritative
  independently observed certificate for every batch-record-copy-method-
  custodian tuple and closed primary, backup, replica, and unknown-location
  residual state.

## Review gates and handoff

- Render exact review gates, open blocker equality, next named owner, and only
  `blocked`, `ready-for-owner-action`, or `complete-reconciled`. Keep
  compliance `not-claimed` and certification
  `evidence-reconciled-not-certified`.

## Authority boundaries

- Do not interpret schedules or law, classify, issue or release holds, grant
  exceptions, approve, accept risk, delete, destroy, move, transfer, mutate
  owner systems, contact anyone, publish, declare compliance, or certify
  disposition. Keep raw content, personal data, confidential details, and
  secrets outside the artifact.
