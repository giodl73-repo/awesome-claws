# Document Renewal Ledger

Use this template for the Document Renewal Tracker X3 artifact.

## Scope

- Owner: `{{ledger.owner}}`
- As of: `{{ledger.asOf}}`
- Privacy mode: `{{ledger.privacyMode}}`

## Sources

List every official page, renewal notice, receipt, portal export, owner note,
appointment note, school or employer note, licensing-board page, and travel
constraint with freshness and privacy labels. Preserve stale, missing,
conflicting, identity-sensitive, dependent-sensitive, account-sensitive, and
travel-sensitive evidence as review gaps.

## Documents

Record document kind, holder, authority, redacted label, expiration date,
expiration state, privacy label, and source references. Do not expose full
document numbers, addresses, birth dates, biometric details, dependent details,
or travel plans unless the owner approved that output.

## Renewal Windows And Materials

Show renewal-open dates, due dates, urgency, required materials, missing
evidence, and conflicts. Keep readiness source-backed and do not infer
eligibility or official acceptance.

## Owner Handoff

Return review questions and prohibited actions. The owner retains authority for
forms, submissions, payments, appointments, agency contact, uploads, account
changes, eligibility claims, and legal, immigration, tax, medical, licensing,
or identity decisions.
