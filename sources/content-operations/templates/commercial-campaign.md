# Commercial campaign draft

## Brief

Record the audience, intended action, supported message, creative proposal,
channel assets, accountable owner, dependencies and explicit review gaps.
Distinguish proposed creative from assets actually produced.

## Channel copy

Write the complete email, web paragraph or other requested text as separate
versioned drafts. Preserve the audience, voice and channel constraints.
Use current permitted claims only. Withhold unsupported performance claims and
unpermitted quotes while continuing supported writing. Do not embed private
review notes or source excerpts in external-facing copy.

## Measurement handoff

Name the metric owner, numerator, denominator, event sources, deduplication,
exclusions, attribution window, timezone, missing-data treatment and when the
window is complete. Mark proposed choices for owner agreement. No supplied
observations means no measured result; a target is not evidence of uplift.

## Review record

Use publication-readiness-record.schema.json without a new contract. Bind
claims and criteria to each exact asset version and list missing approvals.
Deliver the actual drafts alongside the record. No publishing, sending,
scheduling, spending, audience activation or implied approval.
