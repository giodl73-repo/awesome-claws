## Authority and source revisions

List every applicant, authorized helper, school official, testing service,
recommender, institution, admissions office, and financial-aid office with its
exact scope and current status. Preserve the complete source revision chain,
controlled reference, issuer, subject, assertion time, retrieval time, and
freshness. Never include credentials, portal secrets, raw government
identifiers, or unnecessary applicant data.

## Institutions, programs, and requirements

For every application, render the stable institution and program references,
cycle, round, current state, exact requirement set, deadlines with timezones,
and cited current sources. Keep missing, stale, conflicting, waived, optional,
submitted, and received states distinct. Do not infer eligibility, fit,
admission likelihood, aid, or transfer credit.

## Applicant-authored material revisions

Show every essay, statement, short answer, resume, activity record, portfolio,
and writing-sample revision with exact applicant authorship and supersession.
The Claw may organize and compare supplied revisions; it must not write or
materially rewrite applicant-authored content or claim authorship.

## Independent records

Render transcripts, test records, recommendations, school reports, interviews,
fee waivers, and aid records with exact third-party issuer, application scope,
source, state, and gaps. An applicant note is never a substitute for an
independent school, testing-service, recommender, institution, or aid record.

## Submissions and receipts

Separate proposed, prepared, attempted, receipted, failed, and withdrawn
submissions. A submitted state requires the exact application, submitting
human, attempt time, owner or third-party evidence, and an independent
same-submission institution receipt. Do not submit, upload, contact, schedule,
pay, certify, or change any account.

## Official decisions and enrollment responses

Render only exact current official decision evidence from the named institution
or financial-aid authority. Keep admissions, aid, transfer-credit, waitlist, and
enrollment decisions distinct. Never predict, interpret, accept, decline, pay a
deposit, or commit enrollment.

## Gaps and exact review indexes

List every open gap with its exact application, subjects, evidence, and next
owner. Render the exact open-gap, incomplete-application,
missing-requirement, unreceipted-submission, unresolved-decision-application,
and blocked-action indexes. Any mismatch or semantic invalidity keeps the
handoff blocked.

## Prohibited authority

The artifact must keep applicant-content authorship, contact, upload,
submission, scheduling, payment, certification, account change, offer response,
deposit, enrollment, advice, and prediction fields structurally false. The
review must keep authorship, admission-prediction, eligibility, aid,
transfer-credit, fit, submission, and enrollment-commitment claims false.
