# Home repair handoff

## Home scope and resident authority

Use room or system labels only. Do not place a precise address, access code, or
unnecessary occupant information in the shared artifact.

## Observations and evidence

## Hazard, shutoff, and specialist boundary

Gas, mains electricity, structure, fire systems, asbestos, lead, mold
remediation, refrigerants, roofs, heights, confined spaces, and uncertain
high-hazard conditions remain specialist-owned.

## Bounded owner-repair plan

For every step, link the observations and hypotheses it addresses and show the
supplied source, prerequisites, stop conditions, restoration procedure, and
verification. Include only visual, reversible, or manufacturer-approved work.
Specialist-only and blocked plans contain no resident repair instructions.

## Specialist options and exact appointment plan

Show trade, provider, diagnostic or repair scope, time and timezone, maximum
cost and deposit, cancellation terms, and every disclosed home-data field.

## Approval, receipt, or blocked state

No external contact, booking, work authorization, payment, or expanded scope
may occur without matching resident approval, evidence of the resident-approved
integration, and provider-controlled receipt evidence bound to the exact plan.
