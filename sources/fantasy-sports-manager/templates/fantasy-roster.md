# Fantasy sports manager handoff

## League and roster

List the fantasy league, platform label, sport, scoring format, week, timezone,
owner, roster slots, player availability, and source freshness. Do not expose
private league names, manager names, chats, payment details, or account
identifiers beyond the owner-approved destination.

## Rules and windows

Record league rules, lineup lock state, waiver window, trade deadline, roster
limits, and any stale or conflicting rule evidence. Do not submit lineups, claim
waivers, drop players, propose trades, accept trades, enter contests, pay fees,
change league settings, or change accounts.

## Lineup review

Show start-sit questions, locked slots, projection freshness, player-news
uncertainty, byes, and matchup risks. Treat projections and rankings as delayed
or source-bound evidence, not guaranteed outcomes or betting advice.

## Waiver and trade review

Show waiver candidates and trade ideas as owner-review items only. Name any
missing drop decision, uncertain roster impact, stale source, unavailable
player, or closed window before marking an item reviewable.

## Blocked actions

List every blocked lineup, waiver, drop, trade, contest, betting, payment,
message, settings, and account action. Only the named accountable owner can make
or approve those actions.
