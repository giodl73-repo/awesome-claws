# Wardrobe Plan

Use this template for the Wardrobe Organizer X3 artifact.

## Scope

- Owner: `{{plan.owner}}`
- As of: `{{plan.asOf}}`
- Privacy mode: `{{plan.privacyMode}}`

## Sources

List each wardrobe source with kind, freshness, and privacy label. Preserve
stale, missing, conflicting, photo-sensitive, body-adjacent, and
travel-sensitive evidence as review gaps.

## Items

For each item, record category, owner, location, size state, fit state, care
state, privacy label, and source references. Do not infer body, health, or
sensitive identity details from clothing evidence.

## Outfits And Packing

Summarize outfit candidates, event fit, packing lists, care blockers, and
gaps. Keep travel details and photo references minimized unless the owner
approves a broader view.

## Care, Alterations, And Gaps

Separate laundry, dry-cleaning, repair, alteration, owner-check, and gap
questions. Keep appointments, cleaner/tailor messages, pickups, purchases,
returns, resale listings, donations, and disposal as blocked or
exact-approval-bound actions.

## Owner Handoff

Return review questions and prohibited actions. The owner decides purchases,
sales, donations, returns, photo sharing, public posting, messaging, booking,
account changes, body-adjacent disclosure, and professional advice.
