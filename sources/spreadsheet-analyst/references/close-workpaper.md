# Close workpapers

Prepare the actual supporting schedule and concise variance explanation for one
account, entity, currency, period and supplied accounting basis. Preserve the
source workbook and add a review copy. Use `templates/close-workpaper.md`, retaining
the existing spreadsheet-change manifest rather than creating a close coordinator.

Bind opening/closing balances and each supporting schedule to their source and
revision. A required missing schedule, blank amount, duplicate reference or
mismatched entity, period, currency or basis is unresolved evidence, not zero.
Keep real zero values. Calculate closing less opening, sum supported schedule
changes, and show the unexplained difference. Never fill it with a fabricated
accrual, journal or cause. State opening/closing dates and sign conventions.

Consume an account reconciliation only after its existing schema and semantic
validation at the explicit review timestamp. Keep every residual's account, side,
row, amount, reason and next owner. Do not net separate-account exceptions away
when the main schedule or portfolio totals balance. Entity and accounting-basis
bindings must be supplied by the owner when absent from the reconciliation format;
do not claim that a record digest proves those external facts.

Deliver the workbook, source-backed schedule/narrative, exact-source change
manifest and accountant questions. An unchanged version label is not proof that
source bytes are unchanged. Changed scope, amount or source revision invalidates
the relevant review. Record the actual recalculation engine and checks; a cached
result or generated preview is not proof of native Excel execution.

The packaged August example is synthetic and historical as of September 2, 2026.
The complete repository example and actual XLSX files are in
`candidates/accounting-workpapers/` in awesome-claws. Binary workbooks are not
packaged through the text resource loader. Use the existing XLSX capability where
available; otherwise report that workbook production remains unperformed.

The example leaves USD 500 unexplained and retains two operating-account residuals
of USD 1.25 and USD -0.90. Its blocked change manifest is intentional. Source
authenticity, accounting-policy judgment, tax, journal posting, funding, system
mutation, communication, certification and closing the books remain outside this
workpaper's authority. Financial Analyst may use the supported narrative for
analysis without upgrading it to an accounting decision.
