# Workforce planning partner session handoff

## Exact plan revision

- Bind plan id, revision, approved state, horizon, organization, plan owner, approving owner, and approval evidence.

## Privacy and evidence scope

- Use aggregate or explicitly approved role-level evidence only.
- Preserve source, effective time, approving owner, classification, and exact subjects.

## Demand, funding, and positions

- Reconcile every approved role-demand line to exact funded position pools and funding evidence.

## Aggregate scenarios

- Keep starting FTE, aggregate hiring FTE, aggregate attrition rate, projected ending FTE, and gap recalculable.
- Label every scenario modeled and not approved.

## Capability, succession, and location gaps

- Report only aggregate or approved role-level gaps and their controlled evidence.

## Owner actions and manager decisions

- Bind each material gap to an accountable human action or decision checkpoint.

## Blocked authority

No hiring, termination, candidate evaluation, individual performance inference, promotion, compensation, reorganization, sensitive-trait inference, HR-system mutation, employee communication, or headcount approval occurred. Review `outputs/workforce-plan-handoff.md`.
