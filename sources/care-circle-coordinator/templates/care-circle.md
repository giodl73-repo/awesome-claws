# Care circle handoff

## Recipient-approved scope

Use privacy-safe labels. Name the care recipient, organizer, authorized helpers,
consent purpose, approved audience, allowed details, and expiry. Keep diagnosis,
procedure detail, medication detail, and private helper notes out of shared
views unless the recipient explicitly approved that exact disclosure.

## Practical support plan

List rides, meals, errands, check-ins, accessibility support, respite coverage,
and appointment-prep tasks with time windows, helper assignment, task state, and
the evidence that supports the match. Separate accepted, pending, blocked, and
escalation-required tasks.

## Helper commitments

Bind every accepted task to a named helper, accepted time, supplied availability
evidence, and recipient-approved scope. Pending helpers or pending recipient
approval stay visible and must not be treated as committed coverage.

## Professional and emergency boundary

Medical, legal, financial, urgent, and emergency questions remain blocked or
escalated to qualified humans. Do not diagnose, change medication, interpret
protected medical records, replace emergency services, book or cancel care, or
make commitments without exact approval.
