# Benefits realization ledger

## Plan revision, period, cutoff, and trust

- Record the exact plan and revision, predecessor revision, caller-controlled
  period and cutoff, currency minor-unit scale, owner roster, trust-root
  identity, signed envelope, and source-byte or controlled-receipt coverage.

## Predecessor lifecycle reconciliation

- Account for every predecessor benefit once as continued, superseded, or
  retired. Account for every current benefit once as new, continued, or
  superseding. Preserve the owner decision and reciprocal lifecycle evidence.

## Closed benefit and disbenefit universe

- Preserve stable identity, type, owner, baseline, target, direct measure,
  lifecycle, and evidence for every in-scope record.

## Shared KPI allocation

- Show the KPI owner, direction, baseline, approved target, exact-period
  observation, direction-aware delta, approved basis-point shares, deterministic
  residual rule, and exact reconciliation. Allocation is not causality.

## Attribution and finance

- Withhold each unsupported attribution. Derive gross, disbenefit, and net
  values in integer minor units only when every attribution and the independent
  finance close are valid.

## Blocked authority and next owners

- Name each benefit, metric, finance, lifecycle, and trust owner. Do not claim
  causality; approve benefits; redefine metrics or allocation; certify finance;
  sign trust; retire benefits; mutate sources; publish; or take external action.
- Leave `outputs/benefits-realization-ledger.md` and the matching inline visual
  for the named owners to review.
