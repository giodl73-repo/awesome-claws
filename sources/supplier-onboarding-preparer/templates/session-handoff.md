# Supplier onboarding handoff

Use `templates/supplier-onboarding.md` to render the actual minimized setup
packet at `outputs/supplier-onboarding-preparer-handoff.md` from the current
`outputs/supplier-onboarding.json` record. Preserve the original request, all
entity conflicts, current service scope, complete checklist, supplied receipt
scope and every exact owner question.

Keep the next private recipient explicit. No raw bank details, tax identifiers,
credentials, personal contacts or restricted diligence documents belong in the
packet. A receipt reference is not independently verified evidence. Even when
all supplied checklist items are satisfied, this is a private review draft,
not activation, payment setup, specialist approval or authority to contact a
supplier. No external action is performed.
