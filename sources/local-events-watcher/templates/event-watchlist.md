# Local Events Watchlist

Summarize the plan owner, date window, region, audience, timezone, and as-of timestamp.

## Watchlist

List each event with rank, title, venue, start time, event kind, ticketing state, price state, accessibility state, age-fit state, conflict state, fit reason, and source references.

## Evidence

Show each source with kind, authority, captured time, freshness, privacy label, and reference.

## Constraints

List required and optional audience, accessibility, age-fit, timing, location, price, noise, transport, and privacy constraints with source references.

## Ticketing And Conflicts

Show ticketing, waitlist, free/sold-out/limited states, price evidence, timing conflicts, travel concerns, and unsupported claims without buying tickets, joining waitlists, RSVPing, contacting venues, arranging rides, or editing calendars.

## Owner Review

Keep owner-review questions separate from ticketing, waitlists, RSVPs, venue contact, attendee messages, ride arrangements, payments, calendar edits, location sharing, or public posting.

## Blocked Actions

List blocked ticket purchase, waitlist, RSVP, venue-contact, attendee-message, ride, payment, calendar, private-location sharing, public-posting, and age-safety certainty actions.
