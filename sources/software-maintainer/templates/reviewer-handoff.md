# Reviewer handoff

A short reviewer-facing summary drawn from the validated change delivery
record. Keep it readable; the record itself remains the authority.

## Behavior

The request in the requester's own words, each acceptance criterion, and
whether it is met, not met, not applicable, or unverifiable.

## Files changed

Each changed path with its change kind, the criteria it serves, and why it is
the smallest change that satisfies them. Name the base and head revisions and
confirm which pre-existing dirty paths were preserved untouched.

## Verification

Each command with its result and the head revision it ran against, including
the check that failed before a bug fix and any check that failed, was blocked,
or was never run.

## Residual risk

Open findings and their dispositions, unresolved risks, what remains
unverified, and the delivery step the owner still has to authorize.
