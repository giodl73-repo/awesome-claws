# Itinerary and readiness plan

## Trip identity

Name the human traveler owner, origin, destinations, party size, date range,
budget currency and limit, planning timezone, as-of timestamp, and current
draft, blocked, or ready state.

## Public and traveler evidence

List every source with its kind, matching authority, safe public URL or traveler
reference, retrieval time, effective or valid date when relevant, freshness, and
scope. Keep government entry, advisory, and health guidance; official transit,
operator, and venue facts; OpenStreetMap, Open-Meteo, GTFS, and traveler notes
distinct. A map is not evidence of opening hours, accessibility, inventory, or
price.

## Traveler constraints

Trace dates, budget, pace, accessibility or mobility needs, interests, fixed
commitments, lodging and transport preferences, risk tolerance, and other
constraints to traveler-supplied evidence. Mark each constraint required or
optional.

## Day-by-day itinerary

Render days in date order and timed items in local chronological order. Show the
place, source and constraint references, transfer buffer, accessibility notes,
planning status, and a sourced disruption alternative wherever one is required.
Do not hide overlaps, out-of-range dates, or an infeasible transfer.

## Budget range

Show every line item's category, minimum and maximum, currency, inclusion state,
and source references. Distinguish included, contingency, and excluded amounts,
reconcile the total range, and compare the maximum with the traveler-owned
budget limit without presenting planning allowances as live inventory.

## Readiness and final verification

Cover entry or visa, document readiness without sensitive values, health,
safety or advisory, weather, transit, accessibility, opening hours, booking
availability or price, and packing. For each check, show sources, verification
time, status, recheck deadline and state, and notes. Stale, missing, conflicting,
or not-yet-retrieved evidence cannot support a current claim.

## Questions, blockers, and handoff

List every review question and resolution, every blocker and its status, and the
complete day, item, budget, check, question, and blocker references handed to
the named traveler. A ready handoff has no unresolved question or blocker and no
failed or pending mandatory check.

## Prohibited actions

Keep booking, reserving, purchasing, cancellation or modification, check-in,
form submission, provider contact, calendar or message mutation, sensitive
traveler, payment, or verification data submission or storage, term acceptance,
and visa, medical, legal, or safety guarantees explicitly prohibited. The
traveler completes all external actions and final official verification.
