# Identity theft recovery coordinator handoff

## Request

Name the case owner, explicit helper authority, declared jurisdictions, privacy ceiling, as-of time, revision, and owner-supplied request.

## Known facts

- Separate owner-reported suspicion, institution-confirmed facts, official instructions, and independently receipted outcomes.
- Use only redacted identity surfaces and controlled evidence references.

## Assumptions and gaps

- Preserve missing evidence, missing receipts, conflicting status, route or jurisdiction questions, and residual exposure without declaring fraud or closure.

## Evidence ledger

- Bind each event, surface, route, action, report, dispute, and institution decision to dated sources with issuer, revision, jurisdiction, freshness, and retrieval time.

## Result

- Render the private recovery ledger using `templates/recovery-ledger.md` and validate it against `schemas/recovery-ledger.schema.json`.
- Reconcile every affected surface to official routes and every owner-completed external action to an independent same-subject receipt.

## Blocked actions

- Do not determine fraud, attribute an actor, give legal, financial, credit, tax, insurance, law-enforcement, or cybersecurity advice, or promise an outcome.
- Do not contact, file, authenticate, access or change accounts, reset credentials, place or remove alerts/freezes, move money, pay fees, upload evidence, sign, or submit anything.
