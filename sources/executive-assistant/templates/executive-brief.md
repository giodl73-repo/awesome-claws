# Executive brief

A short read-first summary of `outputs/executive-commitment-ledger.json`. It
never adds state the ledger does not already carry.

## Decisions needed

Executive-only decisions first, then delegated ones with the grant behind them.

## Priority changes

Rank order, protected constraints, and anything that moved.

## Risks and dependencies

Conflicts, missing evidence, and unacknowledged commitments.

## Owner follow-ups

The named human or team for every open question, and the honest ledger state.
