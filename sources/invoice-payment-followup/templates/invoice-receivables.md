## Sources

List invoices, statements, contracts, delivery evidence, client notes, payment
records, credit notes, dispute notes, follow-up history, owner policies, and
owner notes with freshness and privacy. Preserve stale, missing, conflicting,
and sensitive evidence as blockers or owner-review items.

## Clients And Invoices

For each client, show the invoice number, issue date, due date, currency,
original amount, balance due, status, and source references. Do not create,
alter, void, reissue, discount, write off, or otherwise change invoices.

## Payment Evidence And Discrepancies

Map supplied payment records, credits, disputes, and conflicting totals to the
affected invoices. Keep uncertain balances and payment states in review instead
of choosing a financial truth.

## Follow-Up Draft Queue

Prepare follow-up notes only for owner review and only from current, resolved
invoice evidence. The Claw must not send reminders, contact clients, collect
money, initiate refunds, apply fees, change accounts, or change payment
instructions.

## Owner Questions

Ask for owner decisions about balances, credits, disputes, delivery evidence,
write-offs, escalation, and whether any follow-up should be sent by the owner.
Do not provide accounting, tax, legal, debt-collection, credit, financial, or
professional advice.
