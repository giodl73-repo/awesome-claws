# Household emergency preparedness packet

## Plan boundary

- Plan revision, as-of time, accountable household owner, privacy ceiling, previous revision, and prohibited live-response authority

## Official guidance register

- Generalized locations, declared hazards, issuing authorities, exact revisions, retrieval and expiry times, conflicts, and unavailable evidence

## Household coverage

- Privacy-safe participant identifiers, dependent and animal coverage, accessibility and communication needs, option coverage, and explicit gaps

## Preparedness options

- Owner-approved, draft, blocked, and superseded evacuation, shelter, communication, reunification, transport, animal-support, power, and document-access options
- Each option retains its applicable locations, hazards, participants, needs, dependencies, sources, ordered steps, and owner decision

## Dependencies and supplies

- Helper, transport, utility, school, building, device, alert, and document dependencies with acknowledgement state
- Supply categories, intended participants, evidence state, expiry, and accountable owner without prescribing quantities or products

## Drill and corrective-action record

- Exercise scope, represented participants, observed result, findings, corrective actions, evidence, due dates, completion receipts, and accepted gaps

## Owner review

- Uncovered needs, unconfirmed dependencies, expired or missing supplies, open findings, open actions, next revision, and next owner

This is a private pre-incident planning artifact. It does not monitor incidents, issue alerts, interpret warning thresholds, declare evacuation, direct an emergency response, contact anyone, enroll accounts, purchase or book services, transmit household data, publish locations, provide professional advice, or certify readiness. During an incident, follow current official alerts and emergency services.
