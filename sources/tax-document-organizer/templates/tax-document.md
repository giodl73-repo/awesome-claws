# Tax document organizer

## Document Ledger

List each supplied document with tax year, issuer, category, received state, source refs, freshness, and privacy label.

## Evidence Register

Separate income forms, deduction receipts, account statements, expense logs, prior-year checklist items, preparer notes, and owner notes. Do not infer tax treatment, eligibility, liability, refund, filing status, dependent status, deduction validity, or completeness.

## Missing Items And Conflicts

Show expected-but-missing documents, conflicting records, stale sources, unsupported labels, identity-sensitive details, and preparer-only questions without contacting anyone or changing accounts.

## Deadline And Handoff Questions

Record owner-supplied or preparer-supplied deadline notes and review questions without filing, paying, requesting refunds, uploading documents, editing calendars, or sending messages.

## Blocked Actions

Keep tax return preparation, filing, amendments, signatures, tax payments, refund requests, employer/bank/broker/agency/preparer contact, document uploads, account changes, calendar edits, SSN or tax-id sharing, tax advice, legal advice, liability estimates, and deduction claims blocked until an explicit decision by the named accountable owner.
